## 🔒 Security — Keeping Your Data Safe

Your business data is precious, like gold! We take security super seriously, and so should you. M4E has robust features to protect your customer information and business operations.

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) with authenticator app 🔐
This is the single most important thing you can do to protect your account!

*   **What it is:** Instead of just a password, 2FA requires a second "proof" that it's really you logging in. It's usually a temporary code generated by an app on your phone.
*   **Why it's important:** If someone somehow gets your password, they *still* can't log in without your phone (and the authenticator app). It's a second, strong lock on your account.
*   **How to Set Up (Recap from Getting Started):**
    *   **Step 1:** Go to "Settings" > "Security."
    *   **Step 2:** Find "Two-Factor Authentication" and click "Enable."
    *   **Step 3:** Download a free authenticator app (like Google Authenticator or Authy) on your smartphone.
    *   **Step 4:** In your authenticator app, choose to "Add Account" and "Scan QR Code." Scan the QR code shown on your M4E screen.
    *   **Step 5:** The app will start generating 6-digit codes that change every 30-60 seconds.
    *   **Step 6:** Enter the current 6-digit code from the app into M4E to confirm.
    *   **Step 7:** Click "Verify & Enable." You're now protected!

### Recovery Codes for Emergencies 🆘

These are your lifeline!

*   **What it is:** A list of unique, one-time-use codes that are generated when you first set up 2FA.
*   **Why it's important:** If you lose your phone, your phone gets stolen, or your authenticator app stops working, you won't be able to generate the 2FA code. These recovery codes are your backup keys to get back into your account.
*   **What to do:**
    *   When you enable 2FA, M4E will display these codes. **DO NOT skip this step!**
    *   **Step 1:** Click "Download" to save them as a text file.
    *   **Step 2:** **Print them out** and keep the paper in a very safe, private place (e.g., a locked drawer, safe).
    *   **Step 3:** You can also save them in a secure password manager that's separate from your regular M4E login.
    *   **DO NOT:** Store them on your device where your authenticator is, or share them with anyone. Each code can only be used once. If you use one, cross it off your list.

### Session Timeout for Safety ⏰

Keep prying eyes away from your business data.

*   **What it is:** M4E will automatically log you out after a certain period of inactivity.
*   **Why it's important:** If you leave your computer or phone unlocked and walk away, someone else might access your M4E account. Session timeout prevents this.
*   **How to manage:**
    *   Go to "Settings" > "Security."
    *   You can set the duration (e.g., 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour) after which your session will time out.
    *   *Recommendation:* For shared computers or public spaces, set a shorter timeout (e.g., 15-20 minutes). If it's your private, secure device, a longer time is fine.

### Team Roles (Owner, Admin, Agent, Viewer) 🧑‍💼

Control who sees and does what in your business.

*   **What it is:** Different levels of access and permissions for your team members.
*   **Why it's important:** You don't want every staff member to have access to sensitive financial information or the ability to delete all your customer contacts! Roles ensure everyone only sees and does what's necessary for their job.
*   **Which role does what (Recap):**
    *   **Owner:** Full control (you!).
    *   **Admin:** Full access to most features and customer data, can manage Agents, but not billing or Owner settings.
    *   **Agent:** Focus on customer interaction (Inbox), sales tools (Pipelines, Debts), and their assigned contacts. Limited access to settings.
    *   **Viewer:** Read-only access to reports and dashboards. No operational changes.
*   **How to assign:** When you invite a new team member (in "Settings" > "Team"), you select their role. You can change their role anytime.

By properly utilizing these security features, you create a safe environment for your business data and ensure that only authorized people can access your M4E Business Growth Engine. Stay safe, stay smart!

Got a question? Stumped on a feature? No need to pull your hair out! We've made it easy for you to find answers and get help when you need it.

### Common Questions

*   **My WhatsApp messages are not sending/receiving!**
    *   **Check 1:** Is your M4E WhatsApp number connected and active in "Settings > WhatsApp"?
    *   **Check 2:** Do you have an active internet connection?
    *   **Check 3:** Are you sending too many messages at once (if not using Broadcasts)? WhatsApp has limits.
    *   **Check 4:** Has your WhatsApp Business API connection expired or been flagged? Check your WhatsApp business account status.
*   **I can't log in after enabling 2FA!**
    *   **Check 1:** Is your authenticator app generating the correct, current 6-digit code? It changes frequently.
    *   **Check 2:** Is your phone's time and date settings correct? Authenticator apps rely on accurate time.
    *   **Check 3:** If you still can't log in, use one of your **Recovery Codes** (refer to Security > Recovery Codes).
*   **My Broadcasts are not delivering to all customers!**
    *   **Reason 1:** Customers must have saved your WhatsApp number to receive non-template messages.
    *   **Reason 2:** Some customers might have blocked your number.
    *   **Reason 3:** Ensure you are using approved WhatsApp Template messages for promotional or non-customer-initiated messages to guarantee delivery (especially important if you're not using official WhatsApp Business API).
*   **How do I update my business bank details for invoices?**
    *   Go to "Settings" > "Account Setup" > "Business Profile" (or similar section depending on exact layout) and look for "Banking Details" or "Invoice Settings."
*   **How can I make my AI Chatbot smarter?**
