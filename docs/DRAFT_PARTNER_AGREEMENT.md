## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- **"Platform"** means the Customer Reactivation Manager software application developed and maintained by M4E, including all updates, patches, and new features released during the Term.
- **"White-Label Instance"** means a deployment of the Platform configured with the Partner's branding, domain, and customisations.
- **"End Client"** means any third party to whom the Partner provides access to the White-Label Instance.
- **"Wholesale Price"** means the fees payable by the Partner to M4E as set out in Schedule A.
- **"Confidential Information"** means all information disclosed by either Party that is marked as confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.
- **"Personal Data"** has the meaning given in the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).
- **"Territory"** means [to be defined per partner — e.g., "the Federal Republic of Nigeria" or "West Africa"].
- **"Term"** means the duration of this Agreement as set out in Clause 8.

---

## 2. GRANT OF LICENCE

2.1 M4E grants to the Partner a **non-exclusive, non-transferable licence** to use, deploy, and sublicense the Platform within the Territory for the duration of the Term.

2.2 The licence includes the right to:
- Deploy the Platform under the Partner's brand identity
- Sublicense access to End Clients
- Set retail pricing at the Partner's discretion
- Provide Tier 1 support to End Clients

2.3 The licence does NOT include the right to:
- Modify, reverse-engineer, or decompile the Platform source code
- Sublicense to other resellers or partners without M4E's written consent
- Use the Platform outside the Territory without M4E's written consent
- Remove or obscure any copyright notices in the Platform's source code

---

## 3. PARTNER OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Support:** The Partner shall provide Tier 1 support to all End Clients, including:
- Responding to End Client enquiries within [Partner's SLA]
- Onboarding new End Clients using M4E-provided materials
- Basic troubleshooting using the Partner Knowledge Base
- Escalating technical issues to M4E in accordance with the support procedures in Schedule B

3.2 **Branding:** The Partner shall:
- Use only approved branding elements on the White-Label Instance
- Not represent the Platform as being developed by the Partner
- Not make claims about the Platform's capabilities that are not supported by M4E's documentation

3.3 **Compliance:** The Partner shall:
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations in the Territory
- Ensure End Clients comply with Meta's WhatsApp Business API terms of service
- Ensure End Clients comply with applicable data protection laws (including NDPA/NDPR in Nigeria)
- Maintain records of End Client consent for data processing

3.4 **Payment:** The Partner shall pay all Wholesale Prices within 7 days of invoice date.

3.5 **Reporting:** The Partner shall provide M4E with monthly reports of active End Clients and their tier levels.

---

## 4. M4E OBLIGATIONS

4.1 **Platform:** M4E shall:
- Maintain the Platform in good working order
- Deploy updates and security patches in a timely manner
- Provide 48 hours' notice before major updates
- Maintain a minimum uptime of 99.5% (excluding scheduled maintenance)

4.2 **Support:** M4E shall:
- Provide Tier 2 support with a 4-business-hour response time
- Provide Tier 3 support with a 1-hour response time for critical issues
- Maintain a Partner Knowledge Base with troubleshooting guides
- Provide initial training as described in Clause 4.3

4.3 **Training:** M4E shall provide the following initial training at no additional cost:
- Platform walkthrough (2 hours)
- Campaign setup and management (2 hours)
- Client onboarding process (1 hour)
- Troubleshooting common issues (1 hour)

4.4 **Infrastructure:** M4E shall manage all server infrastructure, database maintenance, backups, and disaster recovery.

---

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 The Partner shall pay M4E the Wholesale Prices set out in **Schedule A**.

5.2 Invoices are issued monthly in arrears based on active End Client count.

5.3 Payment is due within 7 days of invoice date.

5.4 Late payments attract interest at 2% per month on the outstanding balance.

5.5 M4E reserves the right to suspend the White-Label Instance if payment is more than 30 days overdue, with 7 days' written notice.

---

## 6. DATA PROTECTION AND NDPR COMPLIANCE

6.1 The Partner is the **Data Controller** for End Client data. M4E is the **Data Processor**.

6.2 M4E shall:
- Process Personal Data only on the Partner's documented instructions
- Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures
- Not transfer Personal Data outside Nigeria without the Partner's consent
- Notify the Partner within 72 hours of any data breach
- Delete or return all Personal Data upon termination of this Agreement
- Maintain records of processing activities

6.3 The Partner shall:
- Ensure lawful basis for processing End Client data
- Obtain appropriate consent from End Clients
- Maintain a privacy policy that accurately describes data processing
- Notify M4E of any data subject access requests within 48 hours
- Conduct or commission a Data Protection Impact Assessment where required

6.4 Both Parties shall comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), and any subsidiary legislation.

---

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 M4E retains all intellectual property rights in the Platform, including source code, documentation, and methodologies.

7.2 The Partner retains all intellectual property rights in their brand identity, client relationships, and any content created by the Partner.

7.3 Neither Party acquires any rights in the other Party's intellectual property except as expressly granted in this Agreement.

---

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

8.1 This Agreement commences on the date of execution and continues for an initial term of **6 months** (the "Initial Term").

8.2 After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically on a **month-to-month** basis.

8.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement:
- At the end of the Initial Term, with 30 days' written notice
- After the Initial Term, with 90 days' written notice
- Immediately, if the other Party commits a material breach that is not remedied within 30 days of written notice
- Immediately, if the other Party becomes insolvent or enters administration

8.4 Upon termination:
- The Partner shall cease using the Platform within 30 days
- M4E shall provide End Client data export in standard formats (CSV, JSON) within 14 days of request
- The Partner shall pay all outstanding invoices within 30 days
- Confidentiality obligations survive termination for 2 years

---

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other Party.

9.2 Confidential Information may be disclosed only to employees and contractors who need to know and who are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.

9.3 This obligation does not apply to information that:
- Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party
- Was already known to the receiving Party before disclosure
- Is independently developed by the receiving Party
- Is required to be disclosed by law or regulation

---

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Neither Party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or incidental damages.

10.2 M4E's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Wholesale Prices paid by the Partner in the 12 months preceding the claim.

10.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or breach of data protection obligations.

---

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.1 The Parties shall attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiation.

11.2 If negotiation fails within 30 days, the dispute shall be referred to mediation under the rules of the Lagos Court of Arbitration.

11.3 If mediation fails within 60 days, either Party may commence legal proceedings in the courts of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

11.4 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

## SCHEDULE C: ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

1. Send unsolicited bulk messages (spam)
2. Process data in violation of applicable data protection laws
3. Distribute malware, phishing content, or fraudulent material
4. Violate Meta's WhatsApp Business API terms of service
5. Engage in any illegal activity
6. Attempt to reverse-engineer, decompile, or access the Platform's source code
7. Exceed reasonable usage limits that degrade service for other users
8. Store or transmit content that is defamatory, obscene, or threatening

Violation of this policy may result in immediate suspension of the White-Label Instance.
