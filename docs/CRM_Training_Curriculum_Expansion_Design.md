## 1. GAP ANALYSIS: WHAT'S MISSING

### 1.1 Current Curriculum Coverage Map

The existing 22 modules across 4 levels cover CRM **tool usage** comprehensively. What they do NOT cover is **business impact** — how to translate CRM actions into business outcomes.

| Capability | Current Coverage | Gap |
|-----------|:---:|---|
| CRM navigation & setup | ✅ Full (Modules 1-6) | None |
| Daily operations | ✅ Full (Modules 7-12) | None |
| Advanced features | ✅ Full (Modules 13-18) | None |
| Compliance & safety | ✅ Full (Modules 19-22) | None |
| **Package-specific usage** | ❌ None | Clients don't know which features apply to their package |
| **Report interpretation** | ⚠️ Basic (Module 11) | Module 11 covers WHAT numbers mean, not WHAT TO DO about them |
| **Funnel engine usage** | ❌ None | 5-stage funnel not covered at all |
| **Strategic decision-making from data** | ❌ None | No training on adjusting business strategy based on CRM data |
| **ROI calculation & tracking** | ⚠️ Mentioned (Module 21) | Brief mention, no hands-on calculation exercises |
| **Industry-specific best practices** | ❌ None | All examples are generic; no industry playbooks |
| **Periodic report interpretation** | ❌ None | Clients receive reports but aren't trained to act on them |
| **Competitive benchmarking** | ❌ None | Clients don't know if their numbers are good for their industry |
| **Campaign optimisation from results** | ⚠️ Partial (Module 16) | Covers setup, not iterative improvement from data |
| **Funnel stage diagnostics** | ❌ None | No training on identifying which funnel stage is underperforming |
| **Customer lifetime value understanding** | ❌ None | Critical for retention-focused businesses |
| **Seasonal & cyclical planning** | ⚠️ Mentioned (Module 16) | Brief mention of seasonal campaigns, no planning framework |

### 1.2 The Core Problem

The current curriculum teaches clients to **drive the car** (use CRM features). What's missing is teaching them to **navigate to the destination** (use CRM data to grow their business). A client who completes all 22 modules can:

- ✅ Send broadcasts, run campaigns, manage contacts
- ✅ Read dashboard numbers
- ❌ Decide WHICH campaign to run based on their current business situation
- ❌ Interpret a funnel report and identify the bottleneck
- ❌ Calculate whether their marketing spend is profitable
- ❌ Adjust their messaging based on sentiment analysis trends
- ❌ Know when to shift from reactivation to acquisition
- ❌ Compare their performance against industry benchmarks

### Module 25: Your Growth Engine Package — The Automated Funnel

**Level:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Advanced) | **Time:** 75 minutes | **For:** Package 3 clients

**Learning Objectives:**
- ✅ Understand the 5-stage funnel (Attract → Capture → Nurture → Close → Expand)
- ✅ Know which CRM features power each funnel stage
- ✅ Read your funnel scorecard and identify bottlenecks
- ✅ Understand how industry presets configure your funnel
- ✅ Know the difference between weekly and monthly reports

**Content Outline:**

1. **Opening Story:** Chef Emeka upgraded to Package 3 and was amazed when the system showed him exactly where customers were dropping off — 80% of his ad clicks were captured, but only 30% were converting to orders. The funnel showed him the nurture stage needed work.
2. **The 5-Stage Funnel Explained (with Nigerian examples):**
   - **Attract:** "This is like putting a signboard on a busy road" — ads, social media, SEO bringing people to you
   - **Capture:** "This is like when someone walks into your shop" — they give you their WhatsApp number
   - **Nurture:** "This is like when your sales person talks to them" — automated messages building trust
   - **Close:** "This is when they bring out their wallet" — purchase, order, booking

3. **Your 5 Campaigns + 5 Automations + 3 Flows:** Complete map of what's running
4. **The Funnel Scorecard:** How to read the 5-number scorecard

5. **Finding Your Bottleneck:** "If your funnel was a pipe, where is the leak?"
   - High Attract but low Capture = your landing page or QR code isn't working
   - High Capture but low Nurture = your automated messages aren't engaging
   - High Nurture but low Close = your offer or pricing needs adjustment
   - High Close but low Expand = you're not asking for referrals or reviews
6. **Industry Presets:** How your funnel is configured for your specific industry
7. **Weekly vs Monthly Reports:** What each contains and when to check
8. **Hands-On Exercise:** Open your funnel dashboard, identify your weakest stage, and write down 3 ideas to improve it

### Module 30: Reading Your Campaign Reports Like a Business Owner

**Level:** ⭐⭐⭐ (Intermediate) | **Time:** 60 minutes

**Learning Objectives:**
- ✅ Open and read a campaign report without confusion
- ✅ Understand every metric: sent, delivered, read, replied, converted
- ✅ Calculate campaign ROI in Naira
- ✅ Compare campaigns to find your best performer
- ✅ Decide whether to repeat, adjust, or stop a campaign

**Content Outline:**

1. **Opening Story:** Shade ran 4 campaigns for her salon last month. Two made money, one broke even, and one lost money. But she didn't know which was which until she learned to read the reports.
2. **The Campaign Report Walkthrough:**
   - **Delivery Metrics:** Sent → Delivered → Read → Replied (the message funnel)
   - **Engagement Metrics:** Click rate, reply rate, opt-out rate
   - **Conversion Metrics:** Leads generated, sales made, revenue earned
   - **Cost Metrics:** Messages sent (cost), time spent, ad spend (if applicable)
3. **The Campaign Health Traffic Light:**
   - 🟢 Delivery rate above 95%, read rate above 60%, reply rate above 10%, positive ROI
   - 🟡 Delivery rate 85-95%, read rate 40-60%, reply rate 5-10%, break-even
   - 🔴 Delivery rate below 85%, read rate below 40%, reply rate below 5%, negative ROI
4. **Calculating Campaign ROI:**
   - Simple formula: (Revenue from campaign - Cost of campaign) ÷ Cost of campaign × 100
   - Example: Campaign brought ₦150,000 revenue, cost ₦20,000 → ROI = 650%
   - "For every ₦1 you spent, you got back ₦7.50"
5. **The Repeat-Adjust-Stop Decision:**
   - **Repeat:** ROI positive, engagement high → run it again with same settings
   - **Adjust:** ROI marginal, some metrics good → change the weak parts
   - **Stop:** ROI negative, engagement low → try a different approach
6. **Comparing Campaigns:** Side-by-side comparison technique
7. **Hands-On Exercise:** Pull reports for your last 3 campaigns, calculate ROI for each, and decide: repeat, adjust, or stop

### Module 31: Understanding Your Funnel Report — Where Are Customers Getting Stuck?

**Level:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Advanced) | **Time:** 60 minutes

**Learning Objectives:**
- ✅ Read the 5-stage funnel scorecard
- ✅ Identify your funnel's weakest stage
- ✅ Understand conversion rates between stages
- ✅ Interpret the "what worked" and "needs attention" sections
- ✅ Act on the recommendations section

**Content Outline:**

1. **Opening Story:** Chef Emeka's funnel report showed 1,000 people saw his ads (Attract), 400 clicked to WhatsApp (Capture), 200 had conversations (Nurture), but only 30 ordered food (Close). His funnel was leaking at the Close stage — people were interested but not buying. The report recommended adding a limited-time offer, which doubled his orders.
2. **The Funnel Scorecard Explained:**
   - Each stage shows a number, a label, and a change percentage
   - Positive change (↑) = improving, Negative change (↓) = declining
   - The stages should flow like water: each stage should have fewer people than the previous one, but not TOO many fewer
3. **Healthy Funnel Benchmarks (Nigerian Market):**
   - Attract → Capture: 20-40% conversion ("Out of 100 people who see your ad, 20-40 should click")
   - Capture → Nurture: 50-70% conversion ("Out of 100 who click, 50-70 should start a conversation")
   - Nurture → Close: 10-25% conversion ("Out of 100 conversations, 10-25 should buy")
   - Close → Expand: 15-30% conversion ("Out of 100 buyers, 15-30 should refer or buy again")
4. **Diagnosing Funnel Problems:**
   - Low Attract numbers → Your ads or content aren't reaching enough people
   - Low Capture rate → Your landing page, QR code, or WhatsApp link isn't compelling
   - Low Nurture rate → Your automated messages aren't building trust
   - Low Close rate → Your offer, pricing, or call-to-action needs work
   - Low Expand rate → You're not asking for referrals or reviews at the right time
5. **The Financials Section:**
   - Ad spend: How much you spent on advertising
   - Revenue: How much customers spent
   - ROI multiple: Revenue ÷ Ad spend (should be above 3x)
   - Cost per customer: Ad spend ÷ Number of new customers
   - Average order value: Total revenue ÷ Number of orders
6. **Acting on Recommendations:** The report gives specific suggestions — how to prioritise and implement them
7. **Hands-On Exercise:** Open your latest funnel report, identify the weakest stage, and write an action plan to improve it

### Module 32: Customer Segments — What Your Data Reveals About Your Customers

**Level:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Advanced) | **Time:** 60 minutes

**Learning Objectives:**
- ✅ Understand RFM segmentation (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)
- ✅ Identify your Champions, Loyalists, At-Risk, and Lost customers
- ✅ Know which segment to focus on for maximum revenue impact
- ✅ Create targeted campaigns for each segment
- ✅ Track segment migration over time (customers moving between segments)

**Content Outline:**

1. **Opening Story:** Alhaji Musa discovered that 20% of his customers generated 80% of his revenue. He was spending equal time messaging all customers. When he focused his VIP campaign on the top 20%, his revenue jumped 40%.
2. **The Customer Pyramid:**
   - 🏆 Champions (5-10%): Buy often, spend a lot, refer others → Protect them at all costs
   - 💎 Loyalists (15-20%): Buy regularly, medium spending → Encourage them to spend more
   - 🌱 Promising (20-25%): Recent buyers, growing engagement → Nurture them into loyalists
   - ⚠️ At-Risk (15-20%): Used to buy, slowing down → Win them back before they leave
   - 💤 Dormant (20-30%): Haven't bought in a long time → Reactivation campaign needed
   - ❌ Lost (10-15%): Very old, no engagement → Low priority, occasional check-in
3. **Reading Your Segment Dashboard:**
   - How many customers in each segment
   - Revenue per segment
   - Average order value per segment
   - Segment migration (who moved up or down)
4. **The Segment Action Matrix:**
   - Champions → VIP treatment, exclusive offers, referral requests
   - Loyalists → Upsell, cross-sell, loyalty rewards
   - Promising → Welcome series, product education, first-purchase incentive
   - At-Risk → Win-back campaign, satisfaction check, special offer
   - Dormant → Reactivation campaign, "we miss you" message
   - Lost → Quarterly check-in, major promotion only
5. **Tracking Segment Migration:** "Are customers moving UP the pyramid or DOWN?"
6. **Hands-On Exercise:** Open your segments page, identify your top 10 Champions by name, and send each a personal thank-you message

2. **ROI Calculation Workshop:**
   - Simple ROI: (Revenue - Cost) ÷ Cost × 100
   - Campaign ROI: Revenue attributed to campaign ÷ Campaign cost
   - Channel ROI: Revenue from channel ÷ Total channel spend
   - Package ROI: Total revenue increase ÷ Package cost
3. **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV):**
   - What it is: "How much a customer will spend with you over their entire relationship"
   - How to calculate: Average order value × Purchase frequency × Customer lifespan
   - Why it matters: A customer worth ₦500,000 over 3 years justifies spending ₦50,000 to acquire them
4. **The ROI Dashboard:** Where to find ROI numbers in your CRM
5. **Budget Allocation Framework:**
   - High ROI campaigns → Increase budget
   - Medium ROI campaigns → Optimise before increasing
   - Low ROI campaigns → Fix or stop
6. **Presenting ROI to Stakeholders:** Simple templates for showing marketing value
7. **Hands-On Exercise:** Calculate your CLV, your campaign ROI for last month, and your overall marketing ROI. Present it in a one-page summary.

### Module 36: Tactical Adjustments — When and How to Change Your Approach

**Level:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Advanced) | **Time:** 60 minutes

**Learning Objectives:**
- ✅ Identify signals that require tactical adjustments
- ✅ Know the 7 most common adjustments and when to make them
- ✅ Use A/B thinking to test changes before committing
- ✅ Measure the impact of adjustments
- ✅ Avoid common mistakes when making changes

**Content Outline:**

1. **Opening Story:** Shade's salon campaigns had a 15% reply rate for 3 months, then suddenly dropped to 5%. Instead of panicking, she checked her CRM data, found that her message timing had shifted to evenings (when her customers were busy with family), adjusted back to lunch hours, and reply rates recovered to 18%.
2. **The 7 Adjustment Signals:**
   - 📉 Declining open rates → Adjust message timing or subject lines
   - 📉 Declining reply rates → Adjust message content or call-to-action
   - 📉 Declining conversion rates → Adjust offer, pricing, or urgency
   - 📈 Rising opt-out rates → Reduce frequency, improve targeting
   - 📉 Declining satisfaction scores → Investigate service quality
   - 📉 Declining reactivation rates → Refresh win-back messaging
   - 📈 Rising cost per customer → Optimise ad targeting or switch channels
3. **The Adjustment Playbook:**
   - **Message Timing:** Check your analytics for best send times by day and hour
   - **Message Content:** Compare high-performing vs low-performing messages
   - **Audience Targeting:** Narrow or broaden your segment
   - **Offer Structure:** Test different discounts, bundles, or urgency triggers
   - **Channel Mix:** Shift budget between WhatsApp, ads, and email
   - **Frequency:** Increase or decrease how often you message
   - **Automation Rules:** Adjust trigger conditions and delays
4. **The A/B Thinking Framework:**
   - Change ONE thing at a time
   - Run for at least 2 weeks before judging
   - Compare against the previous period
   - Document what you changed and what happened
5. **Common Mistakes:**
   - Changing too many things at once
   - Judging too quickly (less than 1 week)
   - Ignoring seasonal effects
   - Copying competitors without understanding your own data
6. **Hands-On Exercise:** Identify one declining metric in your CRM, choose an adjustment from the playbook, implement it, and set a reminder to check results in 2 weeks
