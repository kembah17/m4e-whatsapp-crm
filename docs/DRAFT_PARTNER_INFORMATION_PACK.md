Complete the partner application form. We review your business, experience, and target market.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I set my own pricing?**  
A: Yes. The suggested retail prices are guidelines. You set whatever price your market supports.

**Q: Do my clients know about M4E?**  
A: No. The platform appears entirely under your brand. There is no M4E branding visible to your clients.

**Q: What happens if I want to leave the programme?**  
A: You can exit with 90 days' notice. Your clients' data can be exported in standard formats (CSV, JSON). We assist with a smooth transition.

**Q: Can I add custom features?**  
A: Custom development is available at additional cost. Feature requests that benefit all partners may be added to the platform roadmap at no charge.

**Q: How do updates work?**  
A: Updates are deployed automatically. We notify partners 48 hours before major updates. No action required from you.

**Q: What about data ownership?**  
A: Your clients own their data. You have administrative access. M4E processes data on behalf of you and your clients under the partner agreement's data processing terms.

**Q: Do I need technical staff?**  
A: No. Basic computer literacy is sufficient. We handle all technical infrastructure.

**Q: What is the minimum commitment?**  
A: 6-month initial term, then month-to-month. No minimum client count required.
