## About Your Phone Number

You have **two options** for your WhatsApp Business number:

### Option A: Use Your Own Number
- You connect your existing business phone number to the WhatsApp Business API
- We guide you through the process step by step using our setup wizard
- You keep full ownership of the number
- **Best for:** Businesses that already have a known business number customers recognise

### Option B: M4E Provides a Number
- We provide a dedicated WhatsApp Business number for your campaigns
- Zero technical effort from you — we handle everything
- The number is managed through your M4E account
- **Best for:** Businesses that want a separate number for marketing, or do not have a dedicated business line

### Important Things to Know About Your Number

- **You will NOT lose your existing WhatsApp conversations** if you use Option A. Meta's Coexistence feature allows the WhatsApp Business App and the API to work on the same number simultaneously.
- **You CAN still use WhatsApp on your phone** for personal conversations. The API handles automated messages while you continue using the app normally.
- **If your business is not registered with CAC**, we can still work with you. We will discuss alternative verification paths during your consultation.

## About Your Customer Database

We can work with your customer data in **any format**. You do not need to prepare a fancy spreadsheet — just give us what you have:

| Format | How to Send It |
|--------|---------------|
| **Phone contacts** | Forward contact cards to us via WhatsApp |
| **Spreadsheet** (Excel, CSV) | Email or WhatsApp the file to us |
| **Exercise book or paper list** | Take a photo and send via WhatsApp |
| **Text list** | Type or paste names and numbers in a WhatsApp message |
| **Google Contacts** | Export and send the file |
| **POS system export** | Export and send the file |

Our system can read and process all of these formats automatically. We will clean, deduplicate, and organise your contacts before any campaigns begin.

### Import Limits Per Method

| Method | Maximum Per Submission |
|--------|:---:|
| WhatsApp contact cards | 20 contacts |
| VCF file | 5,000 contacts |
| CSV or Excel file | 10,000 contacts |
| Photo of a list | 50 contacts |
| Text message | 30 contacts |
| Email attachment | 10,000 contacts |

You can submit multiple times if you have more contacts than these limits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Before You Sign Up

**Q: How much does it cost?**  
A: Our packages start from ₦2,300,000 for Customer Reactivation. Visit [marketing4effect.vercel.app/pricing](https://marketing4effect.vercel.app/pricing) for full details. We also offer a standalone CRM platform from ₦50,000/month.

**Q: How long before I see results?**  
A: Most clients see their first customer responses within 48 hours of the first campaign. Meaningful revenue impact typically shows within 2-4 weeks.

**Q: What if my business is not registered with CAC?**  
A: We can discuss alternative verification paths. Some businesses can verify through their Facebook Business Page or other documentation. Book a consultation and we will assess your situation.

**Q: Do I need any technical knowledge?**  
A: No. We handle all the technical setup. Your CRM dashboard is designed to be as simple as using WhatsApp itself.

### About WhatsApp

**Q: Will I lose my existing WhatsApp conversations?**  
A: No. Meta's Coexistence feature means you can use the WhatsApp Business App and the API on the same number. Your existing chats remain untouched.

**Q: Can I still use WhatsApp on my phone?**  
A: Yes. The API handles automated campaigns while you continue using the WhatsApp Business App for personal conversations.

**Q: What if I already have a WhatsApp Business account?**  
A: That is fine. We can connect your existing WhatsApp Business number to the API, or set up a separate number for campaigns.

**Q: Are there extra costs for WhatsApp messages?**  
A: Yes, Meta charges per conversation. Marketing messages cost approximately ₦45-75 per conversation (24-hour window). We optimise your campaigns to minimise costs while maximising results. Full pricing transparency is provided in your dashboard.

### About Your Data

**Q: Is my customer data safe?**  
A: Yes. We comply with Nigeria's Data Protection Act (NDPA) and the NDPR. Your data is encrypted, stored securely, and never shared with third parties. You retain full ownership of your data at all times.

**Q: Can I export my data if I leave?**  
A: Yes. You can export all your contacts, conversations, and campaign data at any time in standard formats (CSV, JSON).

**Q: What happens to my data after the engagement ends?**  
A: Your data remains in your CRM account for 90 days after the engagement ends. You can export it at any time during this period. After 90 days, data is securely deleted unless you request an extension.
