## Section 1: Welcome to Your CRM 🎉

Congratulations! You now have access to the **M4E Customer Reactivation Manager** — a powerful tool built specifically for Nigerian businesses like yours. Whether you run a restaurant, a boutique, a salon, a logistics company, or a training academy, this CRM is designed to help you bring back old customers, keep current ones happy, and grow your revenue using WhatsApp, the app your customers already use every day.

### What Does This CRM Do?

Think of the M4E Customer Reactivation Manager as your **digital sales assistant** that never sleeps. It keeps track of all your customers, remembers when they last bought from you, sends them messages at the right time, and helps you turn one-time buyers into loyal, repeat customers.

Here is the simple truth: **it costs 5 to 7 times more to find a new customer than to keep an existing one**. Most Nigerian businesses have hundreds or even thousands of past customers sitting in their phone contacts, WhatsApp chats, and exercise books — customers who bought once and never came back. This CRM helps you reach those customers and bring them back.

### What You Want → How the CRM Helps

| What You Want to Do | How the CRM Helps You |
|---|---|
| Bring back customers who stopped buying | Win-Back Campaign sends automatic messages to dormant customers with special offers |
| Remember customer birthdays | Birthday Campaign auto-sends personalized wishes and discounts on their special day |
| Stop losing online sales | Cart Abandonment Recovery reminds customers to complete their purchase |
| Know which customers spend the most | RFM Scoring automatically ranks customers by how recently, how often, and how much they buy |
| Send promotions to all customers at once | Broadcast feature lets you message hundreds or thousands of customers in one click |
| Get more Google reviews | Post-Purchase Follow-Up asks happy customers to leave reviews |
| Manage customer conversations | WhatsApp Inbox shows all customer chats in one place for your whole team |
| Track sales progress | Sales Pipeline gives you a visual board showing where each deal stands |
| Answer customer questions 24/7 | AI Chatbot handles common questions even when you are sleeping |
| Know if customers are happy or unhappy | Sentiment Analysis reads message tone and alerts you to unhappy customers |
| Run holiday promotions | Seasonal Campaign templates for Eid, Christmas, Easter, Valentine's, and more |
| Grow through word-of-mouth | Referral Program turns happy customers into recruiters who bring new ones |

### How It Works

The CRM works through **three main channels**:

1. **WhatsApp** (Primary) — This is the main channel. Since over 90% of Nigerian smartphone users have WhatsApp, this is where your customers are. The CRM connects to your WhatsApp Business account and lets you send messages, run campaigns, and chat with customers — all from one dashboard.

2. **Email** (Secondary) — For customers who prefer email or for sending detailed information like receipts, reports, and newsletters. The CRM connects to your email through Brevo (a reliable email service).

3. **SMS** (Coming Soon) — For reaching customers who may not have internet access at the moment. This feature is being developed and will be available soon.

## Section 2: Signing Up & Logging In 🔐

### Creating Your Account (First Time)

If your M4E team has already created your account, skip to "Logging In" below. If you need to create a new account, follow these steps:

1. **Open your web browser** (Chrome is recommended) on your computer or phone
2. **Go to** [crm.marketing4effect.com](https://crm.marketing4effect.com)
3. You will see the **M4E login page** with the M4E logo at the top, a dark indigo background, and gold accent colors
4. Click the **"Sign Up"** or **"Create Account"** link below the login form
5. You will see a registration form. Fill in:
   - **Full Name** — Your name as you want it to appear (e.g., "Nkechi Okafor")
   - **Email Address** — Use your business email if you have one (e.g., nkechi@mamankechiskitchen.com)
   - **Password** — Choose a strong password (at least 8 characters, mix of letters and numbers)
   - **Confirm Password** — Type the same password again
6. Click the **"Create Account"** button
7. **Check your email** — You will receive a verification email from M4E. Open it and click the **"Verify Email"** link
8. Once verified, you will be taken to your dashboard. Welcome! 🎉

### Logging In (Returning Users)

1. **Go to** [crm.marketing4effect.com](https://crm.marketing4effect.com)
2. You will see the login page with two fields:
   - **Email** — Type the email you used to sign up
   - **Password** — Type your password
3. Click the **"Sign In"** button
4. You will be taken to your **Dashboard**

### Forgot Your Password?

Don't worry — it happens to everyone! Here is how to reset it:

1. On the login page, click **"Forgot Password?"** (it is below the password field)
2. Enter your **email address** in the box that appears
3. Click **"Send Reset Link"**
4. **Check your email** (also check your spam/junk folder)
5. Click the **reset link** in the email
6. You will see a page where you can type a **new password**
7. Type your new password and confirm it
8. Click **"Reset Password"**
9. You will be redirected to the login page. Log in with your new password

> ⚠️ **Important**: The reset link expires after 1 hour. If it has expired, just request a new one.

## Section 3: Your Dashboard — Understanding Your Numbers 📊

When you log in, the first thing you see is your **Dashboard**. Think of it as the front page of a newspaper about your business — it shows you the most important numbers at a glance.

### Dashboard Numbers Explained

At the top of your dashboard, you will see several cards showing key numbers:

| Dashboard Card | What It Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Total Contacts** | How many customers are in your CRM | "1,247 contacts" means you have 1,247 customer records |
| **Active Contacts** | Customers who have interacted recently (last 30 days) | "342 active" means 342 customers messaged or bought recently |
| **Dormant Contacts** | Customers who haven't interacted in 60+ days | "589 dormant" means 589 customers need reactivation |
| **Revenue Recovered** | Money earned from customers the CRM helped bring back | "₦2,450,000" means your campaigns brought back ₦2.45M in sales |
| **Messages Sent** | Total messages sent this month (WhatsApp + Email) | "3,200 sent" means 3,200 messages went out this month |
| **Open Rate** | Percentage of messages that were read | "89%" means 89 out of 100 messages were opened |
| **Response Rate** | Percentage of messages that got a reply | "34%" means 34 out of 100 messages got a customer reply |
| **Active Campaigns** | Number of campaigns currently running | "3 active" means 3 campaigns are sending messages right now |

### Revenue Recovery Chart

Below the number cards, you will see a **Revenue Recovery Chart**. This is a bar chart or line graph that shows how much revenue your campaigns have recovered over time (weekly or monthly). 

- **Green bars** = Revenue recovered that period
- **The trend line** = Shows if your recovery is going up or down over time
- You can hover over any bar to see the exact amount
- Use the date filter at the top right of the chart to change the time period (Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, This Year)

**What good numbers look like**: If your revenue recovery is trending upward month over month, your campaigns are working well. Even a small upward trend is good.

### Contact Status Breakdown

You will also see a **pie chart** (or donut chart) showing how your contacts are distributed:

- 🟢 **Active** — Customers who bought or interacted recently. These are your healthy relationships.
- 🟡 **Warm Dormant** — Customers who haven't interacted in 30-60 days. They are starting to drift away.
- 🟠 **Hot Dormant** — Customers who were very active but suddenly stopped. They need attention now.
- 🔴 **Cold Dormant** — Customers who haven't interacted in 60+ days. They need a win-back campaign.
- ⚪ **New** — Recently added contacts who haven't been categorized yet.

**What to aim for**: You want the green (Active) section to be the biggest. If the red (Cold Dormant) section is growing, it is time to run a win-back campaign.

### Message Performance Metrics

The dashboard also shows your **message performance** for the current period:

| Metric | Good | Warning | Needs Attention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open Rate | 80%+ | 60-80% | Below 60% |
| Response Rate | 25%+ | 15-25% | Below 15% |
| Delivery Rate | 95%+ | 90-95% | Below 90% |
| Opt-out Rate | Below 2% | 2-5% | Above 5% |

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Check your dashboard at least once a day, preferably in the morning. It takes less than 2 minutes and keeps you informed about your business health.

### The Sidebar Menu

On the left side of your screen (or behind a menu icon on mobile), you will see the **sidebar menu**. This is how you navigate to different parts of the CRM:

### Warning Signs on Your Dashboard

Pay attention to these warning signs:

- 🔴 **Dormant contacts growing faster than active contacts** — You are losing customers. Run a win-back campaign immediately.
- 🔴 **Open rate dropping below 60%** — Your messages may not be relevant. Review your message content and targeting.
- 🔴 **Opt-out rate above 5%** — You may be sending too many messages or irrelevant content. Reduce frequency.
- 🟡 **No campaigns running** — You are missing opportunities. At least one campaign should always be active.
- 🟡 **Response rate below 15%** — Your messages are being read but not acted on. Improve your calls-to-action.

## Section 4: Setting Up Your Account ⚙️

Before you start using the CRM to its full potential, take a few minutes to set up your account properly. This is like setting up a new phone — do it once and everything works better.

### Profile Setup

1. Click **Settings** in the sidebar menu (the gear icon ⚙️)
2. You will see the **Profile** tab (it is usually the first tab)
3. Fill in or update these fields:

| Field | What to Enter | Example |

This is the most important setup step. Your CRM needs to be connected to your WhatsApp Business account to send and receive messages.

1. Go to **Settings** → **WhatsApp** tab

3. You will see:
   - **Sender Name** — The name that appears when you send emails (e.g., "Mama Nkechi's Kitchen")
   - **Sender Email** — The email address emails come from
   - **Daily Limit** — How many emails you can send per day (depends on your plan)
   - **Connection Status** — Should show 🟢 Connected

### First Things to Do After Login

Here is your priority checklist for your first session:

1. ✅ **Complete your profile** — Fill in all the fields in Settings → Profile
2. ✅ **Check WhatsApp connection** — Make sure it shows 🟢 Connected

> ⚠️ **Common Mistake**: Skipping the contact import step. The CRM is only as powerful as the data you put into it. Even if you only have 50 customer phone numbers, import them. You can always add more later.

## Section 5: Your WhatsApp Inbox 💬

The WhatsApp Inbox is where you read and reply to all customer messages. Think of it as WhatsApp on your computer, but with superpowers — you can see customer details, assign conversations to team members, use quick replies, and more.

### Opening the Inbox

1. Click **Inbox** in the sidebar menu (the chat bubble icon 💬)
2. You will see a screen split into two parts:
   - **Left side**: A list of all conversations, with the most recent at the top
   - **Right side**: The selected conversation's messages

Each conversation in the list shows:
- The **customer's name** (or phone number if no name is saved)
- A **preview** of the last message
- The **time** of the last message
- A **badge** showing unread message count (if any)
- A **label** showing the conversation status (Open, Pending, Resolved, Spam)

### Reading Messages

1. Click on any conversation in the left panel
2. The full conversation history appears on the right
3. You will see:
   - **Customer messages** on the left side (with a light background)
   - **Your replies** on the right side (with a colored background)
   - **Timestamps** below each message
   - **Message status icons** next to your sent messages

### Message Status Icons

| Icon | Meaning | What It Looks Like |
|---|---|---|
| 🕐 Clock | Message is being sent | A small clock icon |
| ✓ Single grey tick | Message sent to WhatsApp servers | One grey checkmark |
| ✓✓ Double grey ticks | Message delivered to customer's phone | Two grey checkmarks |
| ✓✓ Double blue ticks | Message read by customer | Two blue checkmarks |
| ❌ Red X or circle | Message failed to send | A red error icon |

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: If you see a ❌ failed icon, don't panic. It usually means the customer's phone was off or they have no internet. The message will often deliver later. If it keeps failing, check if the phone number is correct.

### Replying to Messages

You have several ways to reply:

**Text Reply:**
1. Click in the **message input box** at the bottom of the conversation
2. Type your message
3. Press **Enter** or click the **Send** button (arrow icon)

**Send an Image:**
1. Click the **attachment icon** (📎 paperclip) next to the message input box
2. Select **"Image"**
3. Choose an image from your computer or phone
4. Add a caption if you want (optional)
5. Click **Send**

**Send a Document:**
1. Click the **attachment icon** (📎)
2. Select **"Document"**
3. Choose a file (PDF, Word, Excel, etc.)
4. Click **Send**

**Send a Voice Note:**
1. Click and hold the **microphone icon** 🎤 next to the send button
2. Speak your message
3. Release to send (or slide left to cancel)

### Reactions

You can react to customer messages with emojis:
1. Hover over (or long-press on mobile) a customer's message
2. Click the **emoji icon** that appears
3. Select a reaction (👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🙏)
4. The reaction appears below the message

### Quick Replies

Quick replies are pre-written messages you can send with one click. They save you time when you answer the same questions repeatedly.

1. Click the **quick reply icon** (⚡ lightning bolt) in the message input area
2. You will see a list of your saved quick replies
3. Click one to insert it into the message box
4. Edit it if needed, then send

Examples of useful quick replies:
- "Thank you for your message! We will get back to you within 30 minutes."
- "Our business hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm. We will respond when we open."
- "Here is our price list: [link]"
- "Your order has been confirmed! Expected delivery: [date]"

### Template Picker

For sending the first message to a customer (or re-opening a conversation after 24 hours), you need to use an **approved WhatsApp template**:

1. Click the **template icon** (📋) in the message input area
2. Browse or search your approved templates
3. Select a template
4. Fill in any variable fields (like customer name or order number)
5. Click **Send**

> ⚠️ **Important**: WhatsApp requires you to use approved templates when starting a new conversation or replying after 24 hours of inactivity. This is a WhatsApp rule, not an M4E rule. See Appendix L for more about templates.

### Conversation Labels

Every conversation has a label that shows its status:

| Label | Color | Meaning | When to Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Open** | 🟢 Green | Active conversation needing attention | New messages that need a reply |
| **Pending** | 🟡 Yellow | Waiting for something (customer reply, info, etc.) | You replied and are waiting for the customer |
| **Resolved** | ⚪ Grey | Conversation is complete | Issue solved, question answered, sale made |
| **Spam** | 🔴 Red | Unwanted or irrelevant messages | Spam, wrong numbers, or irrelevant messages |

To change a conversation's label:
1. Open the conversation
2. Click the **label dropdown** at the top of the conversation
3. Select the new label

### Assigning Conversations to Team Members

If you have a team (Professional or Business plan), you can assign conversations to specific team members:

1. Open the conversation
2. Click the **"Assign"** button at the top
3. Select a team member from the dropdown
4. The team member will be notified and the conversation will appear in their assigned list

This is useful when:
- A customer asks a technical question that only one team member can answer
- You want to distribute workload evenly among your team
- A specific team member has a relationship with that customer

### Tips for Managing Your Inbox

1. **Reply quickly** — Customers expect fast replies on WhatsApp. Aim for under 30 minutes during business hours.
2. **Use labels consistently** — Mark conversations as Resolved when they are done. This keeps your inbox clean.
3. **Use quick replies** — Set up quick replies for your most common questions. It saves hours every week.
4. **Check unread messages first** — The inbox shows unread count badges. Start with those.
5. **Don't leave customers hanging** — If you cannot solve their issue immediately, send a quick "We are looking into this and will get back to you within [time]" message.

### Example: Mama Nkechi Managing Lunch Orders

Every morning, Mama Nkechi opens her CRM inbox on her phone. She sees 15 new messages from customers ordering lunch. Here is how she handles them:

1. She opens each conversation and reads the order
2. For standard orders, she uses her **quick reply**: "Thank you for your order! Your food will be ready by [time]. Total: ₦[amount]. Please confirm."
3. For special requests, she types a custom reply
4. She marks confirmed orders as **Pending** (waiting for pickup/delivery)
5. After the customer picks up or receives delivery, she marks the conversation as **Resolved**
6. At the end of the day, she has a clean inbox with zero unread messages

This process takes her about 20 minutes in the morning instead of the 2 hours she used to spend scrolling through WhatsApp on her phone.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: If you get a lot of messages, use the **search bar** at the top of the inbox to find specific conversations. You can search by customer name, phone number, or message content.

> ⚠️ **Common Mistake**: Ignoring the "Resolved" label. If you never mark conversations as resolved, your inbox will be cluttered with old conversations and you will miss new ones.

## Section 6: Managing Your Contacts 👥

Your contacts are the heart of your CRM. Every customer, every lead, every person who has ever bought from you or shown interest in your business — they all live here. The better you organize your contacts, the more money you will make.

### What Are Contacts?

A **contact** is any person whose information is stored in your CRM. Each contact has:

- **Name** — Their full name
- **Phone Number** — Their WhatsApp number (this is the most important field)

### Importing Contacts via CSV (Bulk Import)

If you have many customers to add (from a spreadsheet, exercise book, or another system), you can import them all at once using a CSV file.

**What is a CSV file?** It is a simple spreadsheet file that programs like Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers can create. CSV stands for "Comma-Separated Values."

**Step-by-step import process:**

1. **Prepare your spreadsheet** in Excel or Google Sheets with these columns:
   - Column A: **first_name** (e.g., "Chioma")
   - Column B: **last_name** (e.g., "Adeyemi")
   - Column C: **phone** (e.g., "+2348023456789" — no spaces, include +234)
   - Column D: **email** (e.g., "chioma@email.com")
   - Column E: **tags** (e.g., "VIP, Lekki" — separate multiple tags with commas)
   - Column F: **birthday** (e.g., "1990-03-15" — use YYYY-MM-DD format)
   - Column G: **total_spent** (e.g., "150000" — just the number, no ₦ symbol)
   - Column H: **last_purchase_date** (e.g., "2026-01-15")

2. **Save as CSV**: In Excel, go to File → Save As → choose "CSV (Comma delimited)" as the file type. In Google Sheets, go to File → Download → Comma-separated values (.csv)

3. **In the CRM**, go to **Contacts** → click **"Import"** button

4. **Upload your file**: Click "Choose File" or drag and drop your CSV file

5. **Map your columns**: The CRM will show you a preview of your data and ask you to match each column to the correct field:
   - Column A → First Name
   - Column B → Last Name
   - Column C → Phone
   - And so on...

6. **Review the preview**: Check that the data looks correct. The CRM will show you the first few rows.

7. **Handle duplicates**: Choose what to do if a phone number already exists:
   - **Skip** — Don't import the duplicate (recommended)
   - **Update** — Overwrite existing data with new data
   - **Create new** — Create a separate contact (not recommended)

8. Click **"Start Import"**

9. **Wait for completion**: The CRM will process your file. For large files (1,000+ contacts), this may take a few minutes. You will see a progress bar.

10. **Review results**: After import, you will see a summary:
    - ✅ Contacts imported successfully
    - ⚠️ Contacts skipped (duplicates)
    - ❌ Contacts with errors (invalid phone numbers, etc.)

> ⚠️ **Common Mistakes with CSV Import**:
> - **Wrong phone format**: Phone numbers must start with +234 (or your country code). "08023456789" will not work — use "+2348023456789"
> - **Extra spaces**: Make sure there are no extra spaces before or after phone numbers
> - **Wrong date format**: Use YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2026-03-15), not DD/MM/YYYY
> - **Special characters in names**: Avoid using commas in names (since CSV uses commas as separators). "Nkechi, Mrs" should be just "Nkechi"
> - **File too large**: If your file has more than 10,000 rows, split it into smaller files

## Section 7: Understanding Customer Scores (RFM) 📈

### What Is RFM?

RFM stands for **Recency, Frequency, Monetary**. It is a scoring system that tells you how valuable each customer is to your business. The CRM calculates this score automatically — you do not need to do any math.

Think of it like a school report card for your customers:

- **R = Recency** — How recently did they buy? (A customer who bought yesterday is more valuable than one who bought 6 months ago)
- **F = Frequency** — How often do they buy? (A customer who buys every week is more valuable than one who bought once)
- **M = Monetary** — How much do they spend? (A customer who spends ₦500,000 is more valuable than one who spends ₦5,000)

### How Each Score Works

Each factor (R, F, and M) is scored from **1 to 5**:

**Recency Score (R):**
| Score | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Bought very recently (last 7 days) | Chioma bought jollof rice yesterday |
| 4 | Bought recently (8-30 days ago) | Emeka bought a shirt 2 weeks ago |
| 3 | Bought somewhat recently (31-60 days ago) | Fatima visited the salon 6 weeks ago |
| 2 | Haven't bought in a while (61-120 days ago) | Tunde last ordered 3 months ago |
| 1 | Haven't bought in a long time (120+ days ago) | Blessing last visited over 4 months ago |

**Frequency Score (F):**
| Score | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Buys very often (10+ times) | Chioma orders lunch 3 times a week |
| 4 | Buys often (6-9 times) | Emeka buys clothes every month |
| 3 | Buys sometimes (3-5 times) | Fatima visits the salon quarterly |
| 2 | Bought a few times (2 times) | Tunde has ordered twice |
| 1 | Bought only once | Blessing came once and never returned |

**Monetary Score (M):**
| Score | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Spends a lot (top 20% of customers) | Chioma has spent ₦450,000 total |
| 4 | Spends above average | Emeka has spent ₦200,000 total |
| 3 | Spends average | Fatima has spent ₦80,000 total |
| 2 | Spends below average | Tunde has spent ₦25,000 total |
| 1 | Spends very little | Blessing spent ₦3,000 once |

### What the Combined Score Tells You

The three scores are combined into an **overall RFM score** from 3 (lowest: 1+1+1) to 15 (highest: 5+5+5). Here is what different score ranges mean:

| RFM Score Range | Customer Type | What It Means | What to Do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **13-15** | 🌟 Champions | Your best customers. They buy often, recently, and spend a lot. | Reward them! VIP offers, early access, referral requests |
| **10-12** | 💎 Loyal Customers | Great customers who buy regularly | Keep them engaged with exclusive content and offers |
| **7-9** | 👍 Potential Loyalists | Good customers who could become great | Nurture them with personalized recommendations |
| **5-6** | 🆕 New or Average | New customers or occasional buyers | Welcome sequences, product education, gentle nudges |
| **3-4** | 😴 At Risk / Lost | Customers who rarely buy or have stopped | Win-back campaigns with strong incentives |

### How to Use RFM Scores for Targeting

RFM scores make your marketing much smarter:

1. **VIP Campaigns** → Target customers with RFM score 13-15
2. **Win-Back Campaigns** → Target customers with RFM score 3-6
3. **Referral Requests** → Ask customers with RFM score 10+ (they love you enough to recommend you)
4. **Upsell Campaigns** → Target customers with high Frequency but low Monetary (they buy often but spend little — show them premium options)
5. **Re-engagement** → Target customers with high Monetary but low Recency (they used to spend big but stopped — find out why)

### Example: GlowUp Salon Identifying VIP Clients

GlowUp Salon has 1,200 clients. Using RFM scores, they discovered:

- **47 Champions** (RFM 13-15): These clients visit every 2 weeks, spend ₦30,000+ per visit, and have been coming for over a year. GlowUp sends them exclusive early access to new treatments and birthday packages worth ₦50,000.
- **156 Loyal Customers** (RFM 10-12): These clients visit monthly and spend ₦15,000-₦30,000. GlowUp sends them loyalty rewards and seasonal promotions.
- **312 At Risk** (RFM 3-4): These clients visited once or twice months ago and never came back. GlowUp runs a win-back campaign offering 30% off their next visit.

The result? In the first month, GlowUp recovered 89 of the 312 at-risk clients (a 28.5% win-back rate), generating ₦1,335,000 in recovered revenue.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: You do not need to understand the math behind RFM scoring. The CRM calculates everything automatically based on your customer data. Just focus on using the scores to target the right customers with the right messages.

> ⚠️ **Common Mistake**: Treating all customers the same. A customer with an RFM score of 15 should get very different treatment than one with a score of 3. Use the scores!

## Section 8: Your Sales Pipeline 📊

### What Are Pipelines?

A **pipeline** is a visual way to track deals, orders, or customer journeys as they move through different stages. Think of it like a board game — each customer (or deal) is a card that moves from left to right as they progress.

If you have ever used a physical board with sticky notes arranged in columns (like "To Do", "In Progress", "Done"), that is exactly what a pipeline looks like — but digital and much more powerful.

### How It Looks

When you click **Pipeline** in the sidebar, you will see a **kanban board** — a screen divided into vertical columns, each representing a stage. Each column contains **cards** representing individual deals or customers.

You can **drag and drop** cards from one column to the next as deals progress.

### Default Pipeline Stages

The CRM comes with a default reactivation pipeline with these stages:

| Stage | Column Position | What It Means | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Data Import** | 1st (leftmost) | Customer data has been imported but not yet processed | 500 contacts just imported from CSV |
| **Segmentation** | 2nd | Customers have been categorized by tags, RFM, and status | Contacts sorted into VIP, Dormant, New groups |
| **Campaign Active** | 3rd | A reactivation campaign is running for these customers | Win-back messages being sent to 200 dormant customers |
| **Follow-up** | 4th | Customers responded and need personal follow-up | 45 customers replied to the win-back message |
| **Won Back** | 5th | Customer has been successfully reactivated (made a purchase) | 28 customers made a new purchase |
| **Lost** | 6th (rightmost) | Customer did not respond to any reactivation attempts | 172 customers did not respond after 3 attempts |

You can customize these stages or create entirely new pipelines for different purposes.

### Managing Deals

**Moving a deal to the next stage:**
1. Find the deal card in the current column
2. Click and hold the card
3. Drag it to the next column
4. Release (drop) it

The card will now appear in the new column, and the move is recorded with a timestamp.

**Viewing deal details:**
1. Click on any deal card
2. A detail panel opens showing:
   - Contact information
   - Deal value (₦ amount)
   - Current stage
   - Stage history (when it moved through each stage)
   - Notes and activities
   - Associated messages

### Adding Deals Manually

1. Click the **"+ Add Deal"** button at the top of the pipeline
2. Fill in:
   - **Deal Name** — e.g., "Chioma Adeyemi - Catering Order"
   - **Contact** — Select the contact from your list
   - **Value** — The deal amount in Naira (e.g., ₦150,000)
   - **Stage** — Which stage to place it in (usually the first stage)
   - **Notes** — Any relevant details
3. Click **"Create Deal"**
4. The deal card appears in the selected stage column

### Pipeline Analytics

At the top of the pipeline page, you will see summary numbers:

| Metric | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Total Deals** | How many deals are in the pipeline |
| **Total Value** | Combined value of all deals (₦) |
| **Won Deals** | Number of deals in the "Won Back" stage |
| **Won Value** | Total value of won deals (₦) |
| **Conversion Rate** | Percentage of deals that reached "Won Back" |
| **Average Deal Value** | Average ₦ value per deal |
| **Average Time to Close** | How long it takes a deal to go from first stage to "Won Back" |

SwiftMove Logistics uses a custom pipeline to track their B2B delivery contracts:

**Their custom stages:**
1. **Inquiry** — A business contacted them about delivery services

5. **Active Delivery** — Deliveries are in progress

## Section 10: Sending Broadcasts 📢

### What Are Broadcasts?

A **broadcast** is a message you send to many customers at once. Think of it like a group message, but each customer receives it as a private, individual message — they do not see other recipients and they do not know it was sent to many people.

Broadcasts are perfect for:
- 📢 Announcing a flash sale or promotion
- 🎄 Sending holiday greetings (Christmas, Eid, Easter, etc.)
- 🍽️ Sharing daily specials (for restaurants)
- 📦 Announcing new product arrivals
- 📋 Sharing important business updates (new location, changed hours, etc.)
- 🎉 Inviting customers to events

### Broadcast vs Campaign — What Is the Difference?

| Feature | Broadcast | Campaign |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | A single message sent to many people | A series of automated messages over time |
| **Number of messages** | 1 message | Multiple messages (2-5 typically) |
| **Timing** | Sent once (now or scheduled) | Sent automatically over days/weeks |
| **Automation** | Manual — you decide when to send | Automatic — the system sends based on triggers |
| **Best for** | Announcements, flash sales, updates | Win-back sequences, follow-ups, nurture flows |
| **Effort** | Low — write one message and send | Medium — set up once, runs automatically |
| **Example** | "Flash sale! 30% off all items today only!" | Day 1: "We miss you!" → Day 3: "Here's 20% off" → Day 5: "Last chance!" |

**Simple rule**: Use **broadcasts** for one-time announcements. Use **campaigns** for automated sequences that nurture customers over time.

### The 4-Step Broadcast Process

#### Step 1: Choose Your Channel

1. Click **Broadcasts** in the sidebar menu (the megaphone icon 📢)
2. Click **"+ New Broadcast"**
3. Choose your channel:
   - **WhatsApp** — Best for most messages. 90%+ open rate in Nigeria.
   - **Email** — Good for longer content, newsletters, or formal communications.
   - **SMS** — Coming soon. Will be good for reaching customers without internet.

For most Nigerian businesses, **WhatsApp is the best choice**. It has the highest open rates and response rates.

#### Step 2: Write Your Message (or Select a Template)

You have two options:

**Option A: Select a pre-approved template**
1. Click **"Choose Template"**
2. Browse your approved WhatsApp templates
3. Select one and fill in the variable fields (like customer name, offer details, etc.)

**Option B: Write a custom message**
1. Type your message in the message box
2. You can add:
   - **Text** — Your main message
   - **Image** — A product photo, flyer, or promotional image
   - **Document** — A PDF menu, price list, or catalog
   - **Video** — A short promotional video
   - **Buttons** — Call-to-action buttons ("Shop Now", "Call Us", "Visit Website")

> ⚠️ **Important**: For WhatsApp broadcasts, you must use an **approved template** if you are messaging customers who have not chatted with you in the last 24 hours. This is a WhatsApp rule. See Appendix L for details on templates.

**Tips for writing great broadcast messages:**
- Keep it short (under 160 words for WhatsApp)
- Start with something attention-grabbing
- Include a clear call-to-action ("Reply YES to order", "Click here to shop")

### Tips for Better Broadcasts

1. **Do not send too often** — 2-3 broadcasts per week maximum. More than that and customers will opt out.
2. **Always provide value** — Every broadcast should offer something useful: a discount, information, entertainment, or a solution to a problem.
3. **Segment your audience** — Do not send the same message to everyone. A VIP customer should get different treatment than a new customer.
4. **Test before sending** — Send the broadcast to yourself or a team member first to check for typos and formatting.
5. **Track your results** — Check the metrics after every broadcast. If open rates are dropping, change your approach.
6. **Respect opt-outs** — If a customer asks to stop receiving messages, the CRM automatically removes them. Never manually add them back.
7. **Use images wisely** — A good image can double your response rate, but a blurry or irrelevant image can hurt it.

## Section 11: Running Campaigns — Your Money-Making Machine 🎯💰

### What Are Campaigns?

If broadcasts are like shouting an announcement in a market, **campaigns are like having a dedicated sales team** that follows up with each customer personally, automatically, over several days.

A campaign is a **series of automated messages** sent to specific customers based on rules you set. Once you launch a campaign, it runs on its own — sending the right message to the right customer at the right time — while you focus on running your business.

For example, a Win-Back Campaign might work like this:
- **Day 1**: "Hi Chioma! We haven't seen you in a while. We miss you! Here's what's new..."
- **Day 3**: "Chioma, we have a special 20% discount just for you. Valid this week only!"
- **Day 5**: "Last chance, Chioma! Your 20% discount expires tomorrow. Don't miss out!"

All three messages are sent automatically. You set it up once and the CRM does the rest.

### Campaign Gallery

When you click **Campaigns** in the sidebar, you will see the **Campaign Gallery** — a visual display of all available campaign templates. Each template is shown as a card with:

- **Campaign name** and icon
- **Category badge** (Reactivation, Engagement, Retention, Growth, Recovery)
- **Tier badge** (Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3) — indicating which subscription plans include it
- **Brief description** of what the campaign does
- **Expected results** (e.g., "15-25% win-back rate")

You can filter campaigns by category or tier using the filter buttons at the top of the gallery.

### The 6-Step Campaign Launch Process

Launching a campaign follows these six steps:

**Step 1: Analyze** 📊
The CRM analyzes your customer data and shows you:
- How many customers qualify for this campaign
- Their breakdown by status (Active, Dormant, etc.)
- Estimated reach and potential revenue
- Recommended timing

**Step 2: Template** 📝
Choose a campaign template from the gallery (see the 14 templates below) or customize an existing one.

**Step 3: Audience** 👥
Select who should receive the campaign:
- All qualifying contacts
- Filtered by tags, status, branch, or RFM score
- A specific saved segment

**Step 4: Customize** ✏️
Personalize the campaign messages:
- Edit the message text
- Change the timing between messages
- Adjust the offer or discount amount
- Add your business-specific details
- Upload images or attachments

**Step 5: Schedule** 📅
Choose when to start the campaign:
- Start immediately
- Schedule for a specific date and time
- Set the campaign duration (how long it should run)

**Step 6: Review & Launch** 🚀
Review all settings one final time:
- Preview each message in the sequence
- Confirm the audience size
- Check the schedule
- Click **"Launch Campaign"**

## Section 12: Automations ⚡

### What Are Automations?

Automations are rules that tell the CRM: **"When THIS happens, do THAT."** They work like a loyal employee who never forgets, never sleeps, and always follows instructions perfectly.

Instead of manually doing repetitive tasks, you set up the rule once and the CRM handles it automatically from then on.

### Simple Examples

| When THIS Happens (Trigger) | Do THAT (Action) | Why It Helps |
|---|---|---|
| New contact is added | Send a welcome message | Every new customer gets a warm greeting |
| Customer hasn't bought in 30 days | Add "Warm Dormant" tag | Automatically flags customers who are drifting away |
| Customer sends a message with "price" | Reply with price list | Instant response to common questions |
| Customer's birthday is tomorrow | Send birthday message | Never forget a customer's birthday |
| Customer makes a purchase over ₦50,000 | Add "VIP" tag | Automatically identifies high-value customers |
| Customer sends negative message | Alert the manager | Catches unhappy customers before they leave |
| New order from Shopify | Send order confirmation via WhatsApp | Instant order confirmation |
| Customer clicks WhatsApp ad | Start nurture sequence | Automatically follows up with ad leads |

### How to Create an Automation

1. Click **Automations** in the sidebar menu (the lightning icon ⚡)
2. Click **"+ New Automation"**
3. **Name your automation** — Give it a clear, descriptive name (e.g., "Welcome New Customers")
4. **Set the trigger** — Choose what event starts the automation:
   - Contact created
   - Contact updated
   - Tag added/removed
   - Message received
   - Message contains keyword
   - Purchase made
   - Deal stage changed
   - Scheduled time
   - Form submitted
   - Sentiment detected
5. **Set the condition** (optional) — Add extra rules:
   - Only if tag equals "VIP"
   - Only if branch equals "Lekki"
   - Only if purchase amount is greater than ₦10,000
6. **Set the action** — Choose what happens:
   - Send WhatsApp message
   - Send email
   - Add/remove tag
   - Assign to team member
   - Move deal to stage
   - Create a deal
   - Wait (delay before next action)
   - Send notification to team
7. **Add more actions** (optional) — You can chain multiple actions together
8. Click **"Save & Activate"**

## Section 13: AI Chatbot 🤖

### What Is the AI Chatbot?

The AI Chatbot is your **24/7 virtual assistant** that answers customer questions automatically, even when you and your team are sleeping, eating, or on holiday. It uses artificial intelligence to understand what customers are asking and provide helpful answers.

Think of it as hiring a receptionist who:
- Never takes a break
- Knows everything about your business
- Speaks English and Nigerian Pidgin
- Can handle multiple conversations at the same time
- Knows when to call a human for help

**Available on**: Professional plan (100 AI messages/month) and Business plan (unlimited)

### How It Works

Here is the flow of an AI chatbot conversation:

```
Customer sends message
        ↓
AI reads and understands the message
        ↓
┌─── Can AI answer? ───┐
│                       │
│  YES                  │  NO
│  ↓                    │  ↓
│  AI sends answer      │  AI says "Let me connect
│  ↓                    │  you with a team member"
│  Customer satisfied?  │  ↓
│  ↓                    │  Conversation assigned
│  YES → End            │  to human agent
│  NO → Hand off        │
│  to human             │
└───────────────────────┘
```

### What AI Can and Cannot Do

| AI Can Do ✅ | AI Cannot Do ❌ |
|---|---|
| Answer FAQs about your business | Process payments or refunds |
| Share business hours and location | Make decisions about complaints |
| Provide product information and prices | Access external systems (bank, etc.) |
| Explain your services | Guarantee delivery dates |
| Direct customers to the right team member | Negotiate prices |
| Greet customers and collect basic info | Handle sensitive personal information |
| Understand Nigerian English and Pidgin | Replace human empathy for serious issues |
| Handle multiple conversations at once | Make promises on your behalf |
| Work 24/7 without breaks | Understand voice notes or images |

### When AI Hands Off to a Human

The AI is smart enough to know when it cannot help. It will hand off to a human team member when:

1. The customer asks a question not in the Knowledge Base
2. The customer explicitly asks to speak to a human ("I want to talk to a person")
3. The customer expresses strong negative emotion
4. The conversation involves a complaint or refund request
5. The customer asks about something that requires a decision (custom pricing, special requests)
6. The AI has answered 3 questions and the customer is still not satisfied

When handoff happens, the customer sees: "I'll connect you with a team member who can help you better. Please hold on for a moment! 😊"

## Section 16: WhatsApp Forms (Flows) 🔀

### What Are WhatsApp Flows?

WhatsApp Flows are **interactive forms and menus** that appear directly inside the WhatsApp chat. Instead of asking customers to visit a website or fill out a Google Form, you can collect structured information right inside the conversation.

Think of it like this: instead of a back-and-forth conversation where you ask one question at a time ("What service do you want?" → wait for reply → "What date?" → wait for reply → "What time?" → wait for reply), a Flow presents all the questions at once in a clean, app-like form that the customer fills out in seconds.

### What Flows Can Do

|---|---|---|

| **Support Ticket** | Creates structured support requests | Complaint form, issue report |
| **Preference Collection** | Asks customers about their preferences | Style preferences, dietary requirements |

### How Flows Look to Customers

When a customer receives a Flow, they see:

1. A regular WhatsApp message with a **button** (e.g., "Book Now", "Order Here", "Fill Form")
2. They tap the button
3. A **full-screen form** opens inside WhatsApp (it looks like a mini-app)

   - **Checkboxes** (choose multiple options)
5. They fill out the form and tap **"Submit"**
6. They return to the chat with a confirmation message

The entire process takes 30-60 seconds — much faster than a back-and-forth conversation.

## Section 18: Sentiment Analysis 🎭

### What Is Sentiment Analysis?

Sentiment analysis is like having a **mood detector** for your customer conversations. The AI reads your customers' messages and determines whether they are **happy**, **neutral**, or **unhappy** — in real time.

This matters because unhappy customers rarely tell you they are unhappy. They just stop buying. Sentiment analysis catches the warning signs early so you can fix problems before losing customers.

### How It Works

1. Every incoming customer message is analyzed by AI
2. The AI assigns a **sentiment score**: Positive 😊, Neutral 😐, or Negative 😟
3. The sentiment is displayed as a **badge** on the contact's profile
4. Trends are tracked over time (is this customer getting happier or unhappier?)
5. If negative sentiment is detected, alerts and automations can be triggered

### The Sentiment Dashboard

Click **Sentiment** in the sidebar to see:

| Section | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Overall Sentiment** | Pie chart showing % positive, neutral, negative across all conversations |
| **Sentiment Trend** | Line chart showing sentiment changes over time (daily/weekly/monthly) |
| **At-Risk Customers** | List of customers with declining or negative sentiment |
| **Top Issues** | Common themes in negative messages (e.g., "delivery", "quality", "price") |
| **Team Performance** | Sentiment scores by team member (who resolves issues best?) |
| **Recovery Rate** | % of negative-sentiment customers who became positive after intervention |

### Sentiment Badges on Contacts

When you view a contact's profile, you will see a sentiment badge:

| Badge | Meaning | Action Needed |
|---|---|---|
| 😊 **Positive** | Customer is happy and satisfied | Keep doing what you're doing! |
| 😐 **Neutral** | Customer is neither happy nor unhappy | Monitor — could go either way |
| 😟 **Negative** | Customer is unhappy or frustrated | Immediate attention needed |
| 📈 **Improving** | Sentiment is trending upward | Good — your efforts are working |
| 📉 **Declining** | Sentiment is trending downward | Warning — intervene before it gets worse |

### Trend Tracking

The CRM tracks sentiment over time for each customer. This helps you spot patterns:

- **Sudden drop**: A previously happy customer becomes negative → Something specific happened (bad experience, delayed delivery, etc.)
- **Gradual decline**: Sentiment slowly decreasing over weeks → Customer is losing interest or experiencing repeated small issues
- **Recovery**: Negative → Positive → Your intervention worked!
- **Consistently positive**: Your VIP customers who love your business

### Nigerian English and Pidgin Support

The sentiment AI is trained to understand Nigerian English expressions and Pidgin:

| Customer Message | Detected Sentiment | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "This thing sweet well well!" | 😊 Positive | "Sweet well well" = very good |
| "E dey work, no wahala" | 😊 Positive | "No wahala" = no problem |
| "Na wa o, this service no good" | 😟 Negative | "Na wa o" + "no good" = frustration |
| "Wetin be this? I no happy at all" | 😟 Negative | "I no happy" = unhappy |
| "You people are not serious" | 😟 Negative | Common Nigerian expression of frustration |
| "Abeg, when my order go come?" | 😐 Neutral (leaning negative) | Polite but impatient |
| "Thank you, you try well well" | 😊 Positive | "You try" = you did well |
| "I go come back, the food sweet" | 😊 Positive | "I go come back" = I will return |
| "Make una do better abeg" | 😟 Negative | "Do better" = not satisfied |
| "E be like say una no dey hear word" | 😟 Negative | Strong frustration |

### Auto-Escalation for Negative Sentiment

When the AI detects negative sentiment, it can automatically:

1. **Tag the contact** as "At-Risk" or "Complaint"
2. **Assign the conversation** to a manager or senior team member
3. **Send an alert** to the manager's phone
4. **Trigger the Unhappy Customer Recovery campaign** (Campaign #14)
5. **Log the issue** for the monthly satisfaction report

You can configure these auto-escalation rules in **Settings** → **Sentiment** tab.

**Available on**: Business plan

SwiftMove Logistics handles hundreds of delivery conversations daily. Before sentiment analysis, unhappy customers would silently switch to competitors. The team only found out when they saw negative Google reviews.

With Sentiment Analysis:
- The dashboard shows real-time sentiment across all conversations

- The logistics manager gets an alert and personally calls the customer

### Login Issues

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| "Invalid email or password" | Wrong email or password | Double-check your email. Use the "Forgot Password" link to reset. |
| "Account not found" | Using wrong email | Try other email addresses you might have used to sign up. Check your email inbox for the original welcome email from M4E. |

| Reset link expired | Link is only valid for 24 hours | Request a new reset link from the login page. |
| Page not loading | Internet connection issue | Check your internet connection. Try refreshing the page. Clear your browser cache. Try a different browser. |
| "Session expired" | You were logged out for security | This happens after 24 hours of inactivity. Simply log in again. |
| Two-factor code not working | Time sync issue | Make sure your phone's time is set to automatic. If using an authenticator app, try the next code that appears. |
| Locked out after too many attempts | Security protection | Wait 15 minutes and try again. If still locked, contact support. |

### Message Issues

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Messages not sending | WhatsApp disconnected | Go to Settings → WhatsApp tab. Check if the status is green (connected). If red, click "Reconnect". |
| Messages stuck on "Sending" (clock icon) | Slow internet or WhatsApp issue | Wait a few minutes. If still stuck, check your internet connection and try refreshing the page. |
| Messages showing single tick only | Customer's phone is off or no internet | The message was sent but not yet delivered. It will be delivered when the customer comes online. Nothing you can do — just wait. |
| Template message rejected | Template not approved by WhatsApp | Check Settings → Template Manager. If the template status is "Rejected", edit it and resubmit. See Appendix L for template approval tips. |
| "Message failed" (red icon) | Various reasons | Check the error message. Common causes: invalid phone number, customer blocked you, template expired, or WhatsApp API issue. |
| Cannot send images/documents | File too large | WhatsApp has file size limits: Images (5MB), Documents (100MB), Videos (16MB). Compress your file and try again. |
| Customer says they did not receive message | Message may be in their "Requests" folder | If you are messaging someone for the first time, WhatsApp may put your message in their "Message Requests" folder. Ask the customer to check there. |
| Quick replies not showing | No quick replies set up | Go to Settings → Template Manager → Quick Replies tab to create quick replies. |

### Campaign Issues

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign not starting | Schedule set to future date | Check the campaign schedule. If it is set to start in the future, it will start at that time. |
| Campaign shows 0 recipients | No contacts match the criteria | Check the audience settings. Make sure you have contacts that match the selected tags, status, or branch. |
| Campaign paused automatically | Daily sending limit reached | WhatsApp has daily sending limits. The campaign will resume the next day. |
| Low open rates | Messages sent at wrong time | Check when your messages were sent. Try scheduling for 10am-12pm or 4pm-7pm (peak WhatsApp usage in Nigeria). |
| High opt-out rate | Messaging too frequently or irrelevant content | Reduce frequency. Make sure your content is relevant to the audience. Review your targeting. |
| Campaign template says "Pending" | WhatsApp is reviewing the template | Template approval can take 24-48 hours. Wait for approval before launching the campaign. |
| Cannot launch Tier 2/3 campaigns | Plan limitation | Tier 2 campaigns require Professional plan. Tier 3 campaigns require Business plan. Upgrade to access them. |

### Contact Issues

| Contact's recency status seems wrong | Recency thresholds may not match your business | Go to Settings → Recency Settings and adjust the thresholds. Try using an industry preset. |
| Cannot delete a contact | Contact has active conversations | Resolve or close all conversations with this contact first, then try deleting again. |

## Appendix C: Win-Back Campaign Walkthrough 🔄

This is a complete, step-by-step guide to launching your first Win-Back Campaign.

### Before You Start

Make sure you have:
- ✅ At least 20 contacts in your CRM
- ✅ Some contacts who have not purchased in 60+ days (these are your dormant customers)
- ✅ A discount or offer you are willing to give (e.g., 10-20% off)

### Step 1: Open the Campaign Gallery

1. Click **Campaigns** in the sidebar menu (the target icon 🎯)
2. You will see the Campaign Gallery with all available templates
3. Find **"Win Back Dormant Customers"** — it has a 🔄 icon and a "Reactivation" badge
4. Click on it

### Step 2: Review the Analysis

## Appendix G: Contact Import Walkthrough 📥

### Preparing Your Spreadsheet

Before importing, your spreadsheet (Excel or Google Sheets) needs to be formatted correctly.

**Required columns:**

| Column Name | Required? | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| first_name | ✅ Yes | Text | Chioma |
| last_name | Recommended | Text | Okafor |
| phone | ✅ Yes | +234 format | +2348012345678 |
| email | Optional | Email | chioma@email.com |
| tags | Optional | Comma-separated | VIP, Lagos, Regular |
| birthday | Optional | YYYY-MM-DD | 1990-05-15 |
| branch | Optional | Text (must match branch name) | Lekki |
| notes | Optional | Text | Prefers morning appointments |

### Phone Number Formatting

This is the most common source of import errors. Nigerian phone numbers must be in international format:

| Original Format | Correct Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 08012345678 | +2348012345678 | Remove leading 0, add +234 |
| 0812 345 6789 | +2348123456789 | Remove spaces and leading 0, add +234 |
| 234-801-234-5678 | +2348012345678 | Remove dashes, add + |
| +2348012345678 | +2348012345678 | Already correct! |
| 8012345678 | +2348012345678 | Add +234 |

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: In Excel or Google Sheets, you can use this formula to fix phone numbers:
> `="+234"&RIGHT(SUBSTITUTE(A2," ",""),10)` — This takes the last 10 digits and adds +234.

### Date Formatting

Birthdays and dates must be in **YYYY-MM-DD** format:

| Original | Correct | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 15/05/1990 | 1990-05-15 | Day/Month/Year → Year-Month-Day |
| May 15, 1990 | 1990-05-15 | Text → Year-Month-Day |
| 05-15-90 | 1990-05-15 | Use full 4-digit year |

### Step-by-Step Import

1. **Save your spreadsheet as CSV**: In Excel, go to File → Save As → Choose "CSV (Comma delimited)" format. In Google Sheets, go to File → Download → Comma-separated values (.csv)
2. **Go to Contacts** in the CRM sidebar
3. Click **"Import"** button (the upload icon)
4. Click **"Choose File"** and select your CSV file
5. The CRM will show a **preview** of your data with column mapping:
   - It will try to automatically match your columns to CRM fields
   - If a column is not matched, click the dropdown and select the correct field
   - If a column should be skipped, select "Skip this column"
6. Click **"Validate"** — The CRM checks for errors:
   - ✅ Green rows: Ready to import
   - ⚠️ Yellow rows: Warnings (e.g., missing optional fields) — will still import
   - ❌ Red rows: Errors (e.g., invalid phone number) — will NOT import
7. Fix any red errors if possible, or click **"Import Valid Rows"** to import only the green and yellow rows
8. Wait for the import to complete (progress bar shown)
9. You will see a summary: "X contacts imported, Y skipped, Z errors"

### Common Import Errors and Fixes

| Error | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Invalid phone number" | Phone not in +234 format | Fix the phone number format (see table above) |
| "Duplicate contact" | Phone number already exists in CRM | The existing contact will be updated with new data (not duplicated) |
| "Invalid date format" | Birthday not in YYYY-MM-DD format | Fix the date format |
| "File too large" | CSV file exceeds size limit | Split into smaller files (500 contacts per file) |
| "Invalid CSV format" | File is not a proper CSV | Re-save from Excel/Google Sheets as CSV |
| "Missing required field" | No phone number in a row | Add the phone number or remove the row |
| "Encoding error" | Special characters in the file | Save the CSV with UTF-8 encoding |

## Appendix L: WhatsApp Message Templates Guide 📝

### What Are Templates and Why Are They Needed?

WhatsApp requires businesses to use **pre-approved message templates** when starting a new conversation with a customer. This is WhatsApp's way of preventing spam.

**When you need a template:**
- Sending the first message to a customer (they have not messaged you first)
- Sending a broadcast to multiple customers
- Sending a campaign message
- Re-opening a conversation after 24 hours of inactivity

**When you do NOT need a template:**
- Replying to a customer who messaged you first (within 24 hours)
- Continuing an active conversation

### Template Categories

WhatsApp organizes templates into categories:

| Category | What It Is For | Example | Approval Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Marketing** | Promotions, offers, announcements | "Flash sale! 20% off all items today!" | 24-48 hours |
| **Utility** | Order updates, appointment reminders, receipts | "Your order #123 has been shipped" | Usually faster |
| **Authentication** | Login codes, verification | "Your verification code is 1234" | Usually fastest |

### How to Request New Templates

1. Go to **Settings** → **Template Manager**
2. Click **"+ Create Template"**
3. Fill in:

| Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| **Template Name** | A short name (lowercase, underscores, no spaces) e.g., "flash_sale_announcement" |
| **Category** | Marketing, Utility, or Authentication |
| **Language** | English (or other supported languages) |
| **Header** | Optional: Text, Image, Video, or Document |
| **Body** | Your message text with variables like {{1}} for customer name |
| **Footer** | Optional: Small text at the bottom |
| **Buttons** | Optional: Call-to-action or quick reply buttons |

4. Click **"Submit for Approval"**
5. WhatsApp reviews the template (usually 24-48 hours)
6. You will see the status change to:
   - ✅ **Approved** — Ready to use
   - ❌ **Rejected** — Needs changes (see rejection reason)
   - ⏳ **Pending** — Still being reviewed

### Template Approval Process

```
You create template → Submit to WhatsApp → WhatsApp reviews (24-48 hrs)
                                                    ↓
                                          Approved ✅ → Ready to use!
                                          Rejected ❌ → Edit and resubmit
                                          Pending ⏳ → Wait
```

### Tips for Getting Templates Approved

1. **Be clear and specific** — WhatsApp rejects vague templates. Say exactly what the message is about.
2. **Include opt-out option** — Add "Reply STOP to unsubscribe" at the end of marketing templates.
3. **Avoid ALL CAPS** — WhatsApp may reject templates with excessive capitalization.
4. **No misleading content** — Do not promise things you cannot deliver.
5. **Use variables correctly** — Use {{1}}, {{2}}, etc. for personalization. Tell WhatsApp what each variable represents.
6. **Keep it professional** — Avoid slang, excessive emojis, or aggressive sales language.
7. **Match the category** — Do not submit a promotional message as "Utility" — WhatsApp will reject it.
8. **Test with a sample** — Before submitting, read the template as if you were the customer. Does it make sense? Is it helpful?

## Appendix M: RFM Score Interpretation Guide 📊

### What Is RFM?

RFM stands for **Recency**, **Frequency**, and **Monetary** — three dimensions that measure how valuable a customer is to your business:

- **Recency (R)**: How recently did they last buy? (Score 1-5, where 5 = very recent)
- **Frequency (F)**: How often do they buy? (Score 1-5, where 5 = very often)
- **Monetary (M)**: How much do they spend? (Score 1-5, where 5 = high spender)

### Score Breakdown Table

**Recency Score:**

| Score | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Purchased in the last 7 days | Bought yesterday |
| 4 | Purchased in the last 30 days | Bought 2 weeks ago |
| 3 | Purchased in the last 90 days | Bought last month |
| 2 | Purchased in the last 180 days | Bought 4 months ago |
| 1 | Purchased more than 180 days ago | Has not bought in 6+ months |

**Frequency Score:**

| Score | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 20+ purchases | Buys every week |
| 4 | 10-19 purchases | Buys twice a month |
| 3 | 5-9 purchases | Buys monthly |
| 2 | 2-4 purchases | Buys occasionally |
| 1 | 1 purchase | Bought only once |

**Monetary Score:**

| Score | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Top 20% spender | Spends ₦100,000+ total |
| 4 | Top 40% spender | Spends ₦50,000-₦99,999 total |
| 3 | Middle spender | Spends ₦20,000-₦49,999 total |
| 2 | Below average spender | Spends ₦5,000-₦19,999 total |
| 1 | Lowest spender | Spends less than ₦5,000 total |

### Combined Score (0-15)

The combined RFM score is the sum of all three scores:

| Combined Score | Customer Segment | What It Means | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **13-15** | 🏆 **Champions** | Best customers — buy often, spend a lot, bought recently | VIP treatment, exclusive offers, ask for referrals |
| **10-12** | ⭐ **Loyal Customers** | Good customers — regular buyers with decent spending | Loyalty rewards, upsell premium products |
| **8-9** | 💰 **Potential Loyalists** | Recent customers who could become loyal | Nurture with follow-ups, offer loyalty program |
| **6-7** | 🆕 **New Customers** | Just started buying, not yet established | Welcome sequence, great first experience |
| **4-5** | 😴 **At Risk** | Used to be good customers but slowing down | Win-back campaign, special re-engagement offer |
| **2-3** | ❄️ **Hibernating** | Have not bought in a long time, low engagement | Strong win-back offer, "We miss you" campaign |
| **1** | 💀 **Lost** | Bought once long ago, never returned | Last-chance offer, then remove from active lists |

### Action Recommendations for Each Segment

| Segment | Campaign to Use | Message Tone | Offer Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Champions | VIP Exclusive, Referral Program | Grateful, exclusive | Early access, no discount needed |
| Loyal Customers | Loyalty Program, Birthday | Appreciative, rewarding | Moderate discounts, points multiplier |
| Potential Loyalists | Post-Purchase Follow-Up | Encouraging, helpful | Small incentive to return |
| New Customers | Post-Purchase Follow-Up | Welcoming, supportive | First-time buyer discount on next purchase |
| At Risk | Win-Back Dormant | Concerned, inviting | Meaningful discount (15-20%) |
| Hibernating | Win-Back Dormant | "We miss you", nostalgic | Strong discount (20-30%) |
| Lost | Win-Back (last attempt) | Direct, honest | Best offer you can give |

### Visual Guide

Think of your customers as a pyramid:

```
        🏆 Champions (top 10%)
       ⭐ Loyal Customers (15%)
      💰 Potential Loyalists (20%)
     🆕 New Customers (15%)
    😴 At Risk (20%)
   ❄️ Hibernating (15%)
  💀 Lost (5%)
```

Your goal is to move customers UP the pyramid — from Lost to Hibernating, from Hibernating to At Risk, from At Risk to Loyal, and so on.
