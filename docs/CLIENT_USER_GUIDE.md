## 1. Welcome 🎉

### What Is This Tool?

The **M4E Customer Reactivation Manager** (CRM) is your all-in-one tool for managing your customers and making more money from the customers you already have.

Think of it like this: You have a shop full of customers who used to buy from you but stopped coming. This tool helps you:

- 📱 **Reach them on WhatsApp** — where they spend most of their time
- 💬 **Send them the right message** — at the right time, automatically
- 💰 **Bring them back** — with offers they can't resist
- 📊 **See what's working** — so you can do more of it

### What Can It Do For Your Business?

| What You Want | How The CRM Helps |
|---|---|
| "I want customers who stopped buying to come back" | 🔄 **Win-Back Campaigns** automatically message dormant customers with special offers |
| "I want to remind people who abandoned their cart" | 🛒 **Cart Recovery** sends WhatsApp reminders to people who didn't finish buying |
| "I want more positive reviews" | ⭐ **Review Collection** asks happy customers to leave reviews |
| "I want to thank customers after they buy" | 🙏 **Post-Purchase Thank You** sends automatic thank-you messages |
| "I want to keep my best customers happy" | 👑 **VIP Rewards** gives exclusive offers to your top spenders |
| "I want customers to refer their friends" | 🤝 **Referral Program** rewards customers who bring new people |
| "I want to send birthday wishes" | 🎂 **Birthday Campaigns** send personalized wishes with discounts |
| "I want to reduce failed COD deliveries" | 📦 **COD Confirmation** confirms orders before you dispatch |
| "I want to answer customer questions 24/7" | 🤖 **AI Chatbot** answers common questions even when you're sleeping |
| "I want to send announcements to all customers" | 📢 **Broadcasts** send one message to many people at once |

### How Does It Work?

The CRM connects to your **WhatsApp Business** account. When a customer messages you on WhatsApp, it appears in the CRM. When you send a message from the CRM, it goes to their WhatsApp.

You can also send **emails** and (coming soon) **SMS** messages from the same place.

Everything is in one dashboard — your contacts, messages, campaigns, and results.

## 3. Your Contacts 👥

### What Are Contacts?

Contacts are your **customers** — every person who has ever bought from you, messaged you, or been added to your system. Each contact has:

- Their **name**
- Their **phone number** (for WhatsApp)
- Their **email** (if you have it)
- Their **purchase history** (what they bought and when)
- Their **tags** (labels you put on them)
- Their **recency status** (Active, Hot Dormant, Warm Dormant, or Cold Dormant)

### Viewing Your Contacts

1. Click **Contacts** in the sidebar
2. You'll see a list of all your customers
3. Each row shows: Name, Phone, Email, Tags, Status, Last Purchase Date

### Searching and Filtering

- **Search bar** — Type a name, phone number, or email to find someone
- **Filter by tag** — Click the tag filter to show only customers with a specific tag
- **Filter by status** — Show only Active, Hot Dormant, Warm Dormant, or Cold Dormant
- **Filter by branch** — If you have multiple locations, filter by branch

### Adding Contacts

There are three ways to add contacts:

#### Option 1: Add One Contact
1. Click **Contacts** → **Add Contact**
2. Fill in their details (name, phone, email)
3. Add any tags
4. Click **Save**

#### Option 2: Import Many Contacts (CSV)
1. Click **Contacts** → **Import**
2. Download the **template CSV file** (this shows you the correct format)
3. Fill in your customer data in the spreadsheet
4. Upload the file
5. Map the columns (tell the system which column is name, phone, etc.)
6. Click **Import**

> 💡 **Tip:** Make sure all phone numbers start with **+234** (for Nigerian numbers). For example: +2348012345678

#### Option 3: Automatic Import
If you have a Shopify or WooCommerce online store connected, customers are imported automatically when they make a purchase.

### Understanding Tags 🏷️

Tags are like **labels** you stick on customers to organize them. Think of them like folders in your phone.

Examples of useful tags:

| Tag | What It Means |
|---|---|
| `vip` | Your best, highest-spending customers |
| `new-customer` | Someone who just made their first purchase |
| `lagos` | Customer is based in Lagos |
| `interested` | Showed interest in a product but hasn't bought yet |
| `won-back` | Was dormant but came back and purchased again |
| `opted-out` | Asked to stop receiving messages — DO NOT message them |

You can create your own tags that make sense for your business.

### Understanding Recency Status

The system automatically classifies each customer based on when they last bought from you:

| Status | What It Means | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Active** | Bought recently — they're still your regular customer | Green |
| 🟡 **Hot Dormant** | Stopped buying 2-3 months ago — easy to bring back | Yellow |
| 🟠 **Warm Dormant** | Stopped buying 3-6 months ago — needs a good reason to return | Orange |
| 🔴 **Cold Dormant** | Hasn't bought in 6+ months — hardest to bring back | Red |

> 💡 **Why this matters:** The system uses these categories to automatically target the right customers with the right campaigns. Hot Dormant customers get a gentle "we miss you" message, while Cold Dormant customers get a bigger discount to tempt them back.

The exact number of days for each category depends on your industry. A restaurant might consider someone dormant after 30 days, while a furniture store might wait 180 days. Your M4E team has configured the right thresholds for your business.

## 4. Your Inbox 💬

### What Is the Inbox?

The Inbox is where you **read and reply to customer messages**. It works like WhatsApp on your phone, but on your computer — and your whole team can see and respond to messages.

### Reading Messages

1. Click **Inbox** in the sidebar
2. On the left, you see a list of conversations (like your WhatsApp chat list)
3. Click a conversation to open it
4. On the right, you see the full message history with that customer

### Replying to Messages

1. Open a conversation
2. Type your message in the text box at the bottom
3. Press **Enter** or click the **Send** button

> 💡 **Tip:** You can also send images, documents, and voice notes by clicking the attachment icon (📎).

### Message Status Icons

Next to each message you sent, you'll see status icons:

| Icon | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🕐 **Clock** | Message is being sent (pending) |
| ✓ **Single tick** | Message sent to WhatsApp servers |
| ✓✓ **Double tick (grey)** | Message delivered to customer's phone |
| ✓✓ **Double tick (blue)** | Customer has read the message |
| ❌ **Red X** | Message failed to send |

### Conversation Labels

You can label conversations to organize them:

| Label | Use It For |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Conversations that need attention |
| **Pending** | Waiting for something (customer reply, internal decision) |
| **Resolved** | Issue has been handled |
| **Spam** | Unwanted messages |

### Tips for Managing Your Inbox

- **Check your inbox at least 3 times a day** — Morning, afternoon, and evening
- **Reply within 1 hour** during business hours — fast replies build trust
- **Use the AI Chatbot** for after-hours — it can handle common questions while you sleep
- **Assign conversations** to team members if you have a team — so nothing falls through the cracks

## 5. Campaigns — Your Money-Making Machine 🎯💰

This is the most important section of this guide. Campaigns are how you make money with the CRM.

### What Are Campaigns?

Think of campaigns as your **automated sales team**. You set them up once, and they work for you day and night — sending the right message to the right customer at the right time.

Here's a simple analogy:

> Imagine you hired a very smart sales person. You told them: "Go through my customer list. Find everyone who hasn't bought in 3 months. Send them a nice message saying we miss them and give them 15% off. If they don't reply in 2 days, send a reminder. If they still don't reply, send one last message with a bigger offer."
>
> That's exactly what a campaign does — except it does it automatically, never forgets, never gets tired, and works on weekends.

### The Campaign Gallery

To see all available campaigns:

1. Click **Campaigns** in the sidebar
2. Click **New Campaign**
3. You'll see the **Campaign Gallery** — a collection of ready-made campaign templates

Each template is a proven marketing strategy that has been tested and optimized. You don't need to create campaigns from scratch — just pick the one that fits your goal.

### How to Launch Your First Campaign (Step by Step)

Launching a campaign takes about 10 minutes. Here's how:

#### Step 1: Click "New Campaign"
Go to **Campaigns** → **New Campaign**

#### Step 2: Let the System Analyze Your Data
The system will scan your customer list and tell you:
- How many customers you have in each category (Active, Hot Dormant, etc.)
- Which campaign it recommends based on your data
- How many customers will receive the campaign

> 💡 **Tip:** If the system recommends a Win-Back campaign, that usually means you have a lot of dormant customers — which is actually good news because it means there's money waiting to be recovered!

#### Step 3: Choose Your Campaign Template
Browse the gallery and pick a template. Each one shows:
- What it does
- Who it's for
- What results to expect

(See the full list of all 10 templates below)

#### Step 4: Review the Audience
The system automatically selects the right customers for your campaign. You'll see:
- How many customers qualify
- What criteria was used to select them

You can adjust the audience if you want, but the automatic selection is usually best.

#### Step 5: Customize the Messages
The campaign comes with pre-written messages, but you can edit them to match your brand voice. You can:
- Change the wording

## 6. Broadcasts 📢

### What Are Broadcasts?

A broadcast is when you send **one message to many customers at the same time**. Think of it like a megaphone — you say something once, and everyone hears it.

**Use broadcasts for:**
- 🎉 Holiday greetings ("Happy Eid from [Your Business]!")
- 🔥 Flash sales ("50% off everything — today only!")
- 📣 Announcements ("We've moved to a new location!")
- 🎪 Event invitations ("Join us for our grand opening!")
- 📋 Policy updates ("New delivery hours starting Monday")

### How to Send a Broadcast

1. Click **Broadcasts** in the sidebar
2. Click **New Broadcast**
3. Choose your **channel**:
   - 📱 **WhatsApp** — Best for Nigeria (85%+ open rate)
   - 📧 **Email** — Good for longer messages with images
   - 📲 **SMS** — Coming soon
4. Write your **message** (or select a template for WhatsApp)
5. Choose **who receives it**:
   - **All Contacts** — Everyone in your system
   - **By Tags** — Only customers with specific tags (e.g., only "lagos" customers)
   - **By Status** — Only Active, or only Dormant customers
   - **By Branch** — Only customers at a specific location
6. Review the **number of recipients** — make sure it looks right
7. Choose **when to send**:
   - **Send Now** — Goes out immediately
   - **Schedule** — Pick a date and time
8. Click **Send** or **Schedule**

### Checking Your Broadcast Results

After sending, click on the broadcast to see:

| Number | What It Means |
|---|---|
| 📤 **Sent** | Messages that left the system |
| ✅ **Delivered** | Messages that reached the customer's phone |
| 👀 **Read** | Messages the customer opened |
| 💬 **Replied** | Customers who responded |
| ❌ **Failed** | Messages that couldn't be delivered |

### Tips for Better Broadcasts

- ⏰ **Best times to send:** 9-11am and 4-6pm (when Nigerians check their phones most)
- 🎯 **Don't blast everyone** — Send to relevant groups only
- 📝 **Personalize** — Messages with the customer's name get more replies
- 🔄 **Don't overdo it** — Maximum 2-3 broadcasts per week
- 🚫 **Never message opted-out contacts** — It's against the rules and can get your WhatsApp number banned

> 💡 **Broadcast vs Campaign:** A broadcast is a single message to many people. A campaign is a series of messages over several days with tracking. Use broadcasts for announcements, use campaigns for revenue recovery.

## 7. Automations ⚡

### What Are Automations?

Automations are **"set it and forget it" rules** that work while you sleep. You tell the system: "When THIS happens, do THAT" — and it does it automatically, every time.

### Simple Examples

| When This Happens... | Do This... |
|---|---|
| A customer sends a message containing "price" | Automatically reply with your price list |
| A customer is tagged as "interested" | Create a sales deal in your pipeline |
| A customer messages outside business hours | Send an auto-reply: "We're closed, we'll respond tomorrow" |
| A customer replies "YES" to a campaign | Tag them as "responded" and notify your sales team |
| A customer makes a purchase | Tag them as "active" and remove the "dormant" tag |

### How to Create an Automation

1. Click **Automations** in the sidebar
2. Click **New Automation**
3. Give it a **name** (e.g., "Auto-Reply After Hours")
4. Choose the **trigger** — what starts the automation:
   - **Keyword match** — Customer sends a message with specific words
   - **Tag added** — A tag is added to a contact
   - **Message received** — Any message comes in
   - **Time-based** — At a specific time
5. Add the **actions** — what happens when triggered:
   - **Send message** — Reply with a specific message
   - **Add tag** — Put a label on the contact
   - **Remove tag** — Take a label off the contact
   - **Create deal** — Add a deal to your pipeline
   - **Notify team** — Send an alert to a team member
   - **Assign conversation** — Give the chat to a specific person
6. Click **Save**
7. Toggle the automation **ON**

### Your Pre-Built Automations

Your M4E team has already set up some automations for you:

| Automation | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Auto-Tag Campaign Responders** | When someone replies to a campaign with positive words ("interested", "yes", "how much"), they get tagged as "responded" |
| **Escalate Hot Leads** | When someone asks about buying ("buy", "order", "price"), the conversation is assigned to your senior sales person |
| **Out-of-Office Reply** | When someone messages outside business hours, they get an automatic reply |
| **Won-Back Detection** | When a dormant customer makes a purchase, they're automatically tagged as "won-back" |

> 💡 **Tip:** You don't need to create automations yourself — your M4E team can set them up for you. Just tell them what you want to happen automatically.

## 8. Flows 🔀

### What Are Flows?

Flows are **interactive WhatsApp menus** for your customers. Instead of typing long messages back and forth, customers can tap buttons and select from lists — like using an app inside WhatsApp.

### What Can Flows Do?

Here are some examples:

| Flow | What the Customer Experiences |
|---|---|
| **Product Inquiry** | Customer taps "View Products" → sees categories → picks a category → sees products → gets price and details |
| **Appointment Booking** | Customer taps "Book Appointment" → picks a service → picks a date → picks a time → gets confirmation |
| **Order Placement** | Customer picks products → enters quantity → enters delivery address → chooses payment method → gets order confirmation |
| **FAQ Bot** | Customer taps "I need help" → picks a topic → gets the answer → can ask another question |
| **Feedback Collection** | Customer rates their experience (1-5) → leaves a comment → gets a thank you |

### How Flows Look to Your Customers

When a customer enters a flow, they see WhatsApp messages with **buttons** they can tap:


## 9. AI Chatbot 🤖

### What Is the AI Chatbot?

The AI Chatbot is your **24/7 virtual assistant**. It answers customer questions automatically — even at 2am on a Sunday.

Think of it like hiring a very smart receptionist who:
- Never sleeps
- Never takes a break
- Knows everything about your business
- Can handle multiple customers at the same time
- Knows when to call you for help

### How It Works

```
Customer sends a WhatsApp message
        ↓
🤖 AI reads the message and understands what they want
        ↓
📚 Checks your FAQ list for the answer
        ↓
✅ If it knows the answer → sends it automatically
❓ If it's not sure → transfers to a human team member
```

### What the AI Can Do

| ✅ The AI Can... | ❌ The AI Cannot... |
|---|---|
| Answer common questions ("What are your hours?") | Process payments |
| Share your price list | Make business decisions |
| Explain your services | Handle complex complaints |
| Provide your address and directions | Negotiate prices |
| Tell customers about promotions | Access external systems |
| Greet new customers | Replace human judgment |

### When Does the AI Hand Off to a Human?

The AI is smart enough to know when it can't help. It transfers to a human when:

1. 🤔 **It's not confident** — The question is too complex or unusual
2. 🔄 **Too many back-and-forth** — After 3 AI replies, it assumes the customer needs a human
3. 🗣️ **Customer asks for a human** — If they type "agent", "human", or "speak to someone"

When this happens, the customer sees:
> "Let me connect you with a team member who can help you better. Someone will be with you shortly!"

And the conversation appears in your Inbox flagged for attention.

### Teaching the AI About Your Business

The AI learns from your **Knowledge Base** — a list of questions and answers about your business.

**To add a new FAQ:**
1. Go to **AI Chatbot** in the sidebar
2. Click the **Knowledge Base** tab
3. Click **Add Entry**
4. Enter the **question** (how customers ask it)
5. Enter the **answer** (what you want the AI to say)
6. Add **keywords** (words that help the AI find this answer)
7. Click **Save**

**Example entries:**

| Question | Answer | Keywords |
|---|---|---|
| "What are your opening hours?" | "We're open Monday-Friday 9am-6pm and Saturday 9am-1pm." | hours, open, close, time, when |
| "Where are you located?" | "We're at 15 Admiralty Way, Lekki Phase 1, Lagos." | location, address, where, directions |
| "How much is delivery?" | "Delivery within Lagos is ₦2,000. Outside Lagos is ₦3,500." | delivery, shipping, cost, how much |
| "Do you accept transfers?" | "Yes! You can pay via bank transfer to GTBank 0123456789." | transfer, payment, pay, bank |

> 💡 **Tip:** Start with 20-30 FAQs covering your most common questions. Your M4E team can help you set this up. Over time, check the AI conversation logs to see what questions customers are asking that the AI can't answer — then add those to the Knowledge Base.

### Turning the AI On and Off

- Go to **AI Chatbot** in the sidebar
- Toggle the **Enable** switch ON or OFF
- When OFF, all messages go directly to your Inbox for human response
- When ON, the AI handles what it can and transfers the rest

> 💡 **Recommendation:** Keep the AI ON during business hours (it handles routine questions so your team can focus on sales) and especially after hours (so customers get instant responses even when you're closed).

## 11. Pipelines 📊

### What Are Pipelines?

A pipeline is a **visual board** that shows where each customer is in your sales process. Think of it like a board game — each customer moves from one stage to the next until they buy (or don't).

### How It Looks

Imagine columns on a board, with customer cards that move from left to right:

```
| Data Import | Segmentation | Campaign Active | Follow-up | Won Back | Lost |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|-----------|----------|------|
| Adebayo     | Chioma      | Emeka           | Funke     | Ngozi    | Tunde|
| Blessing    |             | Kemi            |           | Chidi    |      |
|             |             | Amara           |           |          |      |
```

### What Each Stage Means

| Stage | What's Happening |
|---|---|
| 📥 **Data Import** | Customer data has been loaded into the system |
| 🏷️ **Segmentation** | Customers have been sorted into groups (Active, Dormant, etc.) |
| 🚀 **Campaign Active** | Campaign messages are being sent to these customers |
| 📞 **Follow-up** | First campaign done, now following up with people who didn't respond |
| ✅ **Won Back** | Customer came back and made a purchase! 🎉 |
| ❌ **Lost** | Customer didn't respond after all attempts |

### Managing Deals

Each card on the board is called a **deal**. A deal represents one customer's journey through your sales process.

**To move a deal:**
- Simply **drag and drop** the card from one column to another
- Or click the card and change the stage in the dropdown

**To add a deal:**
1. Click **Add Deal**
2. Enter the deal name (e.g., "Reactivation - Adebayo")
3. Enter the expected value (e.g., ₦50,000)
4. Link it to a contact
5. Choose the starting stage
6. Click **Save**

> 💡 **Tip:** Most deals are created automatically by your campaigns and automations. You usually don't need to create them manually.

## 15. FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions ❓

### Getting Started

**Q: How do I log in?**
> Go to crm.marketing4effect.com, enter your email and password, and click Sign In. If you forgot your password, click "Forgot password?" to reset it.

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
> Click "Forgot password?" on the login page. Enter your email address. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for a reset link. Click the link and create a new password.

**Q: Can I use the CRM on my phone?**
> Yes! The CRM works in your phone's web browser. Go to crm.marketing4effect.com on Chrome or Safari. It's not an app you download — it's a website that works on any device.

**Q: Can multiple people use the same account?**
> Yes! Go to Settings → Members and invite your team. Each person gets their own login. Don't share passwords — each person should have their own account.

### Contacts

**Q: How do I add my customers to the system?**
> Three ways: (1) Add them one by one — click Contacts → Add Contact. (2) Import many at once — click Contacts → Import and upload a CSV file. (3) Automatically — if you have a Shopify/WooCommerce store connected, customers are added when they buy.

**Q: What format should phone numbers be in?**
> All Nigerian numbers should start with +234. For example: +2348012345678. Remove the leading zero — so 08012345678 becomes +2348012345678.

**Q: What does "dormant" mean?**
> A dormant customer is someone who used to buy from you but hasn't bought recently. "Hot Dormant" means they stopped recently (easiest to bring back). "Cold Dormant" means they stopped a long time ago (hardest to bring back).

**Q: Can I delete a contact?**
> Yes, but be careful — it's permanent. Open the contact, click the menu (⋮), and select Delete. You'll need Admin or Owner role to delete contacts.

**Q: What are tags and why should I use them?**
> Tags are labels you put on customers to organize them. Like putting stickers on folders. For example, tag your best customers as "vip" and your Lagos customers as "lagos". This helps you send targeted messages to the right people.

### Campaigns

**Q: What's the difference between a campaign and a broadcast?**
> A broadcast is ONE message sent to many people (like a flyer). A campaign is a SERIES of messages sent over several days with automatic follow-ups and tracking (like a sales person following a script). Campaigns track revenue — broadcasts don't.

**Q: How much does it cost to send campaign messages?**
> WhatsApp charges per conversation (about ₦30-50 per conversation). Your M4E team can give you exact costs based on your message volume. Email is free up to 300 per day.

**Q: How long does a campaign take to show results?**
> Most campaigns run over 5-7 days (because they have multiple messages with delays between them). Check results after the full sequence has completed. Some customers may take 1-2 weeks to respond.

**Q: Can I run multiple campaigns at the same time?**
> Yes, but be careful not to send too many messages to the same customers. The system will warn you if there's overlap. As a rule, don't run more than 2 campaigns targeting the same audience at the same time.

**Q: What if a campaign isn't working?**
> Check the metrics: If delivery rate is low, there might be a phone number quality issue. If read rate is low, try a different send time. If reply rate is low, the message might need to be more compelling. Talk to your M4E team — they can help optimize.

**Q: What are Tier 1 and Tier 2 campaigns?**
> Tier 1 campaigns (Win-Back, Abandoned Cart, Post-Purchase Thank You, Order Status, Review Collection) are included in all plans. Tier 2 campaigns (COD Confirmation, Birthday, Upsell/Cross-Sell, Referral, VIP Rewards) are available on premium plans or as add-ons.

### Messaging

**Q: Why can't I send a message to a customer?**
> On WhatsApp, you can only send free-form messages within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After 24 hours, you must use an approved template. If you see an error, try using a template instead.

**Q: What's a WhatsApp template?**
> A template is a pre-approved message format. WhatsApp (Meta) requires businesses to get message templates approved before sending them to customers outside the 24-hour window. Your M4E team handles template creation and approval.

**Q: Can I send images and documents?**
> Yes! Click the attachment icon (📎) in the message box to send images, PDFs, or other files.

**Q: What do the tick marks mean?**
> 🕐 Clock = sending, ✓ = sent, ✓✓ grey = delivered, ✓✓ blue = read, ❌ = failed.

### AI Chatbot

**Q: Will the AI say something wrong to my customers?**
> The AI only answers questions it's confident about. If it's not sure, it transfers to a human. You control what it knows through the Knowledge Base. Start with simple FAQs and expand over time.

**Q: Can I turn off the AI chatbot?**

**Q: Does the AI work in Pidgin English?**

### Win-Back Campaign — The Full Story

#### Why Customers Stop Buying

Before we talk about how to bring them back, let's understand why customers leave in the first place:

| Reason | How Common | What You Can Do |
|---|---|---|
| They forgot about you | 🟢 Very common (40%) | Regular reminders and campaigns |
| They found a competitor | 🟡 Common (25%) | Better offers and loyalty programs |
| They had a bad experience | 🟠 Sometimes (20%) | Fix the issue and apologize |
| They no longer need your product | 🔴 Less common (10%) | Introduce new products |
| They moved away | 🔴 Rare (5%) | Offer delivery or online options |

The good news? **65% of dormant customers left for reasons you can fix.** That's what the Win-Back campaign does.

#### The Win-Back Message Sequence

Here's exactly what happens when you launch a Win-Back campaign:

**Day 1 — The "We Miss You" Message:**
```
Hi [Name]! 👋

It's been a while since we saw you at [Business Name]. 
We've been thinking about you and wanted to check in.

We have some exciting new [products/services] we think 
you'll love. Plus, here's a special welcome-back offer 
just for you: [Discount]% off your next purchase!

Use code: COMEBACK[Code]
Valid for 7 days.

Would you like to see what's new? Reply YES! 😊
```

**Day 3 — The Reminder (only if they didn't reply):**
```
Hi [Name]! Just a quick reminder about your exclusive 
[Discount]% discount. It expires in 4 days!

Here are our top picks for you:
🔥 [Product 1] — Now ₦[Price]
🔥 [Product 2] — Now ₦[Price]
🔥 [Product 3] — Now ₦[Price]

Reply to order or ask any questions! 🛍️
```

**Day 5 — The Final Push (only if they still didn't reply):**
```

We'd really love to have you back.

Is there anything we can help with? Maybe you have

Just reply and we'll take care of you! 💛
```

| Metric | What It Means | Target |

- Customer changed their mind but didn't cancel
- Customer doesn't have the cash ready
- Customer gave a wrong address
- Customer placed a duplicate order by mistake

Each failed delivery costs you:
- 🚚 Delivery fee (wasted): ₦1,500-₦3,000

- ⏰ Staff time: ₦500-₦1,000

**Total cost per failed delivery: ₦3,500-₦7,000**

#### What Happens When a Customer Opts Out

When a customer replies "STOP" or asks to be removed:

1. ✅ They are immediately tagged as "opted-out"
2. ✅ All active campaigns stop sending to them

4. ✅ Their data is retained (for your records) but they receive no more messages

> ⚠️ **Important:** Never message an opted-out customer. It's against the law and can result in fines. The CRM automatically prevents this, but if you're sending messages manually outside the CRM, be careful.

## Appendix I: Multi-Branch Management Guide 🏢

If your business has multiple locations, the CRM can manage them all from one dashboard.

### Setting Up Branches

Your M4E team will set up your branches. Each branch has:

| Property | Example |
|---|---|
| **Branch Name** | "Lekki Branch" |
| **Address** | "15 Admiralty Way, Lekki Phase 1" |
| **Phone** | "+2348012345678" |
| **Manager** | "Adebayo Okonkwo" |
| **Operating Hours** | "Mon-Sat 9am-7pm" |

### How Branches Work

- Each **contact** can be assigned to a branch (where they usually shop)
- Each **team member** can be assigned to one or more branches
- **Campaigns** can target specific branches ("Win-Back for Lekki customers only")
- **Reports** can be filtered by branch ("How much revenue did Ikeja generate this month?")
- **Broadcasts** can be sent to specific branches ("Lekki branch is having a sale!")

### Branch-Level Reporting

When you have multiple branches, the dashboard shows:

| Metric | All Branches | Lekki | Ikeja | Abuja |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total Contacts | 1,500 | 600 | 500 | 400 |
| Active | 750 | 320 | 250 | 180 |
| Dormant | 750 | 280 | 250 | 220 |
| Revenue This Month | ₦4.5M | ₦2.0M | ₦1.5M | ₦1.0M |
| Campaign ROI | 45x | 52x | 38x | 41x |

This helps you see which branches are performing well and which need more attention.

### Tips for Multi-Branch Businesses

1. **Assign contacts to the right branch** — When importing, include a "Branch" column
2. **Run branch-specific campaigns** — A Lekki customer should get Lekki-specific offers
3. **Compare branch performance** — Use the reports to identify best practices from top branches
4. **Branch managers should check their inbox daily** — Each branch should have a dedicated person monitoring messages
5. **Centralize strategy, localize execution** — Plan campaigns centrally but customize messages for each location

## Appendix J: Troubleshooting Guide 🔧

Here are solutions to the most common problems:

### Login Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| "Invalid email or password" | Check for typos. Try copy-pasting your password. Use "Forgot password" if needed. |
| "Account locked" | Too many failed login attempts. Wait 30 minutes and try again, or contact M4E support. |
| Page won't load | Check your internet connection. Try a different browser. Clear your cache. |
| "Session expired" | You were logged out for security. Just log in again. |

### Message Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Messages not sending | Check WhatsApp connection status in Settings. Contact M4E if disconnected. |
| Messages showing as "failed" | The phone number might be wrong or the customer blocked you. |
| Template not approved | WhatsApp templates need Meta approval (24-48 hours). Your M4E team handles this. |
| Can't send to a contact | They might be opted out. Check their contact profile for the "opted-out" tag. |
| Images not sending | File might be too large (max 5MB for WhatsApp). Compress the image and try again. |

### Campaign Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Campaign not starting | Check the schedule. Make sure it's set to "Active" not "Draft". |
| Low delivery rate | Phone numbers might be outdated. Run a contact cleanup. |
| No replies | Try different message wording. Check send times. Increase the discount offer. |
| Campaign stuck on "Processing" | Wait 10 minutes. If still stuck, contact M4E support. |

### Contact Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Import failed | Check CSV format. Make sure phone numbers start with +234. Check date formats. |
| Duplicate contacts | Use the deduplication tool in Contacts → Tools → Merge Duplicates. |
| Missing data after import | Check column mapping. Re-import with correct mapping. |
| Can't delete a contact | You need Admin or Owner role. Ask your admin to delete it. |

### General Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Page loading slowly | Clear browser cache. Try Chrome. Check internet speed. |
| Data not updating | Refresh the page (Ctrl+F5). Wait 5 minutes for data to sync. |
| Feature not available | It might be on a higher plan. Contact M4E to discuss upgrading. |
| Something looks broken | Take a screenshot, note what you were doing, and send to M4E support. |

> 💡 **When contacting support, always include:**
> 1. What you were trying to do
> 2. What happened instead
> 3. A screenshot of any error message
> 4. Your account email address
> 5. The time it happened
