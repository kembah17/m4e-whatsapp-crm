# Reactivation Email Sequence

A 6-email sequence designed to win back dormant customers. Send in order with
specified timing. Personalise all `[bracketed]` fields before sending.

---

## Email 1: The 10-Word Email

**Send:** Day 0 (sequence start)
**Goal:** Get a reply. That's it.

**Subject:** Quick question
**Alt subjects:** "Still interested?", "[First name]?"
**Preview text:** I wanted to check in with you about something

**Body:**
```
Hi [First name],

Are you still looking for help with [specific product/service they bought or enquired about]?

[Your first name]
```

**CTA:** Reply to this email
**Notes:**
- No images, no links, no HTML — plain text only
- Feels like a personal email, not marketing
- Expected: 30-40% open rate, 10-15% reply rate
- If they reply YES → fast-track to sales conversation
- If they reply NO → ask what changed, update records

---

## Email 2: The "We Miss You" Email

**Send:** Day 7 (if no response to Email 1)
**Goal:** Rebuild emotional connection

**Subject:** We noticed you've been away, [First name]
**Alt subjects:** "It's been a while", "Checking in on you"
**Preview text:** No pitch, just a genuine hello

**Body:**
```
Hi [First name],

I was going through our records and noticed it's been [X months] since we
last worked together on [specific project/purchase].

I hope everything is going well with [reference to their business/situation].

A lot has changed on our end — we've [mention 1-2 improvements or new
offerings relevant to them]. I thought you might find that interesting.

No pressure at all. I just wanted to say hello and let you know we're
still here if you ever need [core service/product].

Warm regards,
[Your name]
[Your title]

P.S. If there's anything we could have done better last time, I'd
genuinely love to hear about it. Just hit reply.
```

**CTA:** Reply with feedback
**Notes:**
- Warm, personal tone — NOT a marketing email
- The P.S. opens a feedback loop (valuable intel)
- Reference specific details from their history

---

## Email 3: The Exclusive Offer Email

**Send:** Day 14 (if no response to Email 2)
**Goal:** Create urgency with a compelling incentive

**Subject:** Something special for you, [First name] (expires [date])
**Alt subjects:** "A thank-you for being a past customer", "[First name], this is only for you"
**Preview text:** We've set something aside just for returning customers

**Body:**
```
Hi [First name],

Because you're a valued past [customer/client] of [Brand], I wanted to
offer you something we're not sharing publicly:

🎁 [Specific offer: e.g., "20% off your next project", "Free consultation
+ strategy session", "Buy one get one on your next order"]

This is exclusively for customers we've worked with before, and it's
only available until [specific date — 7 days from send].

Here's what [past customers who returned] are saying:

> "[Short testimonial about returning to the brand]"
> — [Customer name, if available]

[CTA Button: Claim Your Exclusive Offer →]

If you have any questions, just reply to this email. I read every response.

Best,
[Your name]
```

**CTA:** Claim offer (link to landing page or reply)
**Notes:**
- Real deadline creates urgency
- Social proof from other returning customers
- "Exclusively for past customers" = VIP treatment
- Track redemption rate to measure sequence effectiveness

---

## Email 4: The Value Reminder Email

**Send:** Day 21 (if no response to Email 3)
**Goal:** Remind them of the value they received

**Subject:** Remember when [specific result they achieved]?
**Alt subjects:** "The results speak for themselves", "[First name], your results from [date]"
**Preview text:** We pulled up your history and found something interesting

**Body:**
```
Hi [First name],

I was looking back at your history with us and wanted to share something:

When we worked together on [specific project/purchase], you [achieved
specific result / received specific benefit]:

📊 [Result 1: e.g., "Increased your website traffic by 45%"]
📊 [Result 2: e.g., "Generated 23 new leads in the first month"]
📊 [Result 3: e.g., "Saved 12 hours per week on manual processes"]

Since then, we've helped [X] other [businesses/customers] achieve
similar results. Here's a recent example:

---
**Mini Case Study: [Client name or industry]**
- Challenge: [Brief problem]
- Solution: [What we did]
- Result: [Specific outcome with numbers]
---

I'd love to help you build on those earlier results. Would you be open
to a quick [15-minute call / chat / visit] to explore what's possible?

[CTA Button: Let's Talk →]

[Your name]
```

**CTA:** Book a call or reply
**Notes:**
- Use REAL data from their account if available
- If no specific data, use general results for their segment
- The case study adds social proof without being pushy

---

## Email 5: The Personal Video Email

**Send:** Day 28 (if no response to Email 4)
**Goal:** Break through with a personal touch

**Subject:** I recorded a quick message for you, [First name]
**Alt subjects:** "30 seconds of my time for you", "[First name], watch this"
**Preview text:** It's only 30 seconds, I promise

**Body:**
```
Hi [First name],

I know your inbox is busy, so instead of another long email, I recorded
a quick 30-second message just for you:

[Video thumbnail image linked to Loom/video]
🎥 Watch my message (0:30)

TL;DW (too long; didn't watch):
- I genuinely miss working with you
- We have [something new/relevant] that I think you'd love
- I'd like to offer you [specific incentive] to reconnect

Just reply "yes" if you're interested, and I'll handle the rest.

[Your name]
```

**Video script (30-60 seconds):**
```
"Hey [First name], it's [Your name] from [Brand]. I wanted to take
30 seconds to reach out personally because I noticed it's been a while
since we connected.

I've been thinking about [something relevant to their situation] and
I think we could really help you with [specific benefit].

We've actually just [launched/improved/created] [something relevant],
and I immediately thought of you.

If you're open to it, I'd love to chat for 15 minutes — no pressure,
just a conversation. Hit reply and let me know. Talk soon!"
```

**CTA:** Reply "yes"
**Notes:**
- Record with Loom (free) — takes 2 minutes
- Use their name in the video for maximum personalisation
- For scale: record one video per segment, not per person
- Thumbnail with play button gets 3x more clicks

---

## Email 6: The Final Chance Email

**Send:** Day 35 (if no response to Email 5)
**Goal:** Last attempt — give them a clear choice

**Subject:** Should I stop reaching out, [First name]?
**Alt subjects:** "One last thing", "I'll take the hint (but first...)"
**Preview text:** I respect your time, so I'm asking directly

**Body:**
```
Hi [First name],

I've sent a few messages over the past few weeks and haven't heard
back. I completely understand — life gets busy.

I don't want to be that company that keeps emailing when you're not
interested. So I'm asking directly:

**Would you like to:**

1️⃣ **Reconnect** — Reply "YES" and I'll set up a time to chat
2️⃣ **Not right now** — Reply "LATER" and I'll check back in 6 months
3️⃣ **Unsubscribe** — Click here to remove yourself from our list

No hard feelings either way. I just want to respect your preference.

If I don't hear back, I'll assume you're busy and I'll reduce my
emails to just [quarterly updates / major announcements].

Thank you for being a [Brand] customer. It genuinely meant a lot.

All the best,
[Your name]
```

**CTA:** Reply with 1, 2, or 3
**Notes:**
- Honesty and respect build long-term brand equity
- "LATER" responses are gold — schedule a 6-month follow-up
- Many people reply to this one because it's refreshingly honest
- Expected: 15-20% response rate on this email alone
- After this: move to quarterly contact only

## Sequence Timing Summary

| Email | Day | Subject Pattern | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. 10-Word | 0 | Quick question | Get a reply |
| 2. We Miss You | 7 | We noticed you've been away | Rebuild connection |
| 3. Exclusive Offer | 14 | Something special for you | Create urgency |
| 4. Value Reminder | 21 | Remember when [result]? | Remind of value |
| 5. Personal Video | 28 | I recorded a message for you | Personal touch |
| 6. Final Chance | 35 | Should I stop reaching out? | Clear choice |

## Key Metrics to Track

- **Open rate** per email (target: 25%+ for Email 1, 20%+ for others)
- **Reply rate** (target: 10%+ for Email 1, 5%+ for others)
- **Click rate** (target: 3%+ for Emails 3-5)
- **Reactivation rate** (target: 10-29% of dormant contacts)
- **Revenue per reactivated customer** (track for 90 days post-reactivation)
