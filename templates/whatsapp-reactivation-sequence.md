# WhatsApp Reactivation Sequence

Four WhatsApp messages designed for the Nigerian market. These complement
the email sequence and should be sent between email touchpoints.

All messages are under 300 characters for optimal readability on mobile.
Personalise all `[bracketed]` fields before sending.

---

## Message 1: The Check-In

**Send:** Day 3 (after Email 1, before Email 2)
**Goal:** Casual reconnection, no selling

```
Hi [First name] 👋

It's [Your name] from [Brand]. How have you been?

I was thinking about you today and wanted to check in. Hope business is going well! 🙏

No agenda — just saying hello.
```

**Notes:**
- Send from a personal WhatsApp Business number, not a broadcast
- If they reply, have a genuine conversation before any pitch
- Nigerian context: "How body?" or "How far?" can replace "How have you been?" for informal brands

**Informal Nigerian variant:**
```
Hi [First name] 👋

Na [Your name] from [Brand]. How far? How body?

I just dey think about you and I wan check on you. Hope say business dey move well! 💪

No wahala — just dey greet you.
```

## Message 2: The Value Drop

**Send:** Day 10 (after Email 2, before Email 3)
**Goal:** Provide value, position as helpful expert

```
[First name], quick tip that might help you 💡

[Share a specific, actionable tip relevant to their industry/need]

We just helped a [similar business type] achieve [specific result] using this approach.

Want me to share more details? No cost, just trying to be helpful 🤝
```

**Example (for a retail client):**
```
[First name], quick tip that might help you 💡

We noticed that businesses sending WhatsApp order confirmations see 23% more repeat purchases than email-only.

We just helped a boutique in Lagos increase repeat orders by 31% with this one change.

Want me to share how they did it? No cost, just trying to be helpful 🤝
```

**Notes:**
- The tip must be genuinely useful, not a disguised pitch
- Reference a real result if possible
- "No cost" removes the sales barrier

## Message 3: The Exclusive Offer

**Send:** Day 21 (after Email 3, before Email 4)
**Goal:** WhatsApp-only deal to drive action

```
[First name] 🎁

I have something special — WhatsApp-only offer for our past customers:

✅ [Specific offer: e.g., "25% off your next order"]
✅ [Bonus: e.g., "Free delivery anywhere in Lagos"]
✅ Valid until [date — 5 days from now]

Just reply "YES" to claim it. I'll sort everything out for you 👍

Only sharing this here, not on email or social.
```

**Notes:**
- "WhatsApp-only" creates exclusivity
- "Reply YES" is the lowest-friction CTA possible
- "I'll sort everything out" = personal service feel
- Nigerian market responds well to exclusivity + personal touch
- Track redemptions separately from email offers

## Message 4: The Voice Note

**Send:** Day 28 (after Email 5, before Email 6)
**Goal:** Maximum personal touch via voice

**Script for voice note (30-45 seconds):**
```
"Hey [First name], it's [Your name] from [Brand]. I'm sending you
this voice note because I wanted to reach out personally.

It's been a while since we worked together and I just wanted to say
we really valued having you as a customer. I know things get busy
but I wanted you to know that [specific thing you can help with]
is still available whenever you're ready.

If you're interested, just send me a quick reply — even just a
thumbsup emoji — and I'll take it from there. No pressure at all.

Hope to hear from you. Take care!"
```

**Accompanying text message (send immediately after voice note):**
```
☝️ Quick voice note for you [First name]

TL;DL: We miss working with you and have [something relevant] whenever you're ready.

Reply 👍 if interested, or just say hi! 😊
```

**Notes:**
- Voice notes have 90%+ listen rates on WhatsApp
- Record fresh for each VIP; use a template recording for bulk
- Nigerian market: voice notes feel more personal than text
- Keep under 45 seconds — respect their time
- The thumbsup emoji CTA is ultra-low friction

## WhatsApp Best Practices (Nigerian Market)

1. **Send during business hours** — 9am-6pm WAT, avoid Sundays
2. **Use WhatsApp Business** — Professional profile with business info
3. **Don't broadcast** — Send individually or use approved templates
4. **Respond quickly** — If they reply, respond within 1 hour
5. **Use voice notes** — Nigerians prefer voice over text for personal messages
6. **Include emojis** — But don't overdo it (2-3 per message max)
7. **Respect opt-outs** — If they say stop, stop immediately
8. **Track in CRM** — Log all WhatsApp interactions
